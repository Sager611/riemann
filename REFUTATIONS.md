# Refutations (claimed proofs checked and found broken; scoped proof-gap reports, not progress on RH)

- Fayed's RH proof breaks at (55): its Appendix Theorem 1 ("L'Hôpital on a disconnected domain") is false — Lean counterexample on the paper's own D_γ; the reconstructed argument is otherwise machine-checked from 4 standard analytic inputs. Reviewer: significant (flaw); machine-check alone: verification milestone. Online: not found. [details](Riemann/Fayed/Theorem1.lean), [proof check](Riemann/Fayed/HARDEN_REPORT.md) (theorem1C_false refutes the printed complex Theorem 1; fayed_gap in Paper.lean locates its use at (55).)
- Desogus's claimed RH proof ("The Three Gates", arXiv 2609.20367v3) breaks at Thm 8.6, ineq. (201): explicit odd test on window log 8 gives Q(v)/⟨e₇,v⟩² ≈ 1.1e-10 (Arb-rigorous) vs claimed ≥ 1.878; bare diagonal Q(e₇) = 1.23 already below. [report](Riemann/Deep/Desogus/REPORT.md) Reviewer: significant. Independent audit (new code + Arb re-run): confirmed [audit](Riemann/Deep/Desogus/AUDIT.md). Online: not found (v3 latest, not withdrawn; Chen referee report and a Sep 2026 critique list gaps only, no counterexample). (Lean: the quantitative core of (201) at k = 7 is refuted conditional on the cited Arb fact Witness7.)
- Ryazanov's claimed RH proof (arXiv 1808.10774v11, Aug 2026) breaks at the step before (4.3): φ*_n is not n!-periodic and does not coincide with χ as claimed — both proved false in Lean for every n ≥ 3 (proofs use a Bertrand prime); the step carries the claimed L² convergence. [report](Riemann/Deep/Ryazanov/REPORT.md) Reviewer: significant (scoped proof-gap refutation; not progress on RH). Online: no critique found.
- Visser, "Primes in short intervals assuming the Riemann hypothesis" (arXiv 2610.27830v1, 20 Oct 2026): Theorem 2 (assuming RH, for every x ≥ 1 and 0 < m ≤ 15.831 the interval [x, x + x^{1/2+1/m}] contains a prime) is false. At x = 7.5, m = 15.831 the interval is [7.5, 10.61], which has no prime; RH plays no role. Cause: §3.2 treats π(n²+n) > π(n²) (checked for n ≤ 7·10^13) as equivalent to π(x+√x) > π(x) for every real x. It is true for real x ≥ 23.14 and for all integers x. Theorem 3 is true but needs a new proof. The same false equivalence appears, harmlessly, in 2610.20058. Reviewer: confirmed (moderate). Novelty: v1 only, no public note found. [report](Riemann/Deep/Visser/REPORT.md)
- "Principle of generating critical points of rational functions and Gauss–Fourier conjecture for second-kind zeros" (arXiv 2610.29790, Oct 2026): the necessity direction of Theorem 0.4 is unproved. The step at l. 1691–1694 (repeated at l. 1700–1702) concludes that a root locus meeting the real axis at M from C* comes from a non-real zero and touches the real axis nowhere else, citing Theorems 3.22 and 3.24; but 3.24 is conditional (a meeting with a real-origin locus at b_{i,k}, then departure into C*), its hypotheses and c_{i,k} = M are never verified, and no lemma excludes other real-axis contacts; the definitions of Γ and "directly" (l. 1237–1253) do not supply this. No counterexample to the statement found (108 integer polynomials of degree 3–4). Reviewer: real gap. [audit](Riemann/Tree/R136.lean), [passages](Riemann/Tree/R137.lean)
