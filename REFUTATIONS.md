Reviewer: significant (flaw); machine-check alone: verification milestone. Online: not found. (201): explicit odd test on window log 8 gives Q(v)/⟨e₇,v⟩² ≈ 1.1e-10 (Arb-rigorous) vs claimed ≥ 1.878; bare diagonal Q(e₇) = 1.23 already below. [report](Riemann/Deep/Desogus/REPORT.md) Reviewer: significant. Independent audit (new code + Arb re-run): confirmed [audit](Riemann/Deep/Desogus/AUDIT.md). - Ryazanov's claimed RH proof (arXiv 1808.10774v11, Aug 2026) breaks at the step before (4.3): φ*_n is not n!-periodic and does not coincide with χ as claimed — both proved false in Lean for every n ≥ 3 (proofs use a Bertrand prime); the step carries the claimed L² convergence. [report](Riemann/Deep/Ryazanov/REPORT.md) Reviewer: significant (scoped proof-gap refutation; not progress on RH). Online: no critique found.
- Visser, "Primes in short intervals assuming the Riemann hypothesis" (arXiv 2610.27830v1, 20 Oct 2026): Theorem 2 (assuming RH, for every x ≥ 1 and 0 < m ≤ 15.831 the interval [x, x + x^{1/2+1/m}] contains a prime) is false. At x = 7.5, m = 15.831 the interval is [7.5, 10.61], which has no prime; RH plays no role. Cause: §3.2 treats π(n²+n) > π(n²) (checked for n ≤ 7·10^13) as equivalent to π(x+√x) > π(x) for every real x. It is true for real x ≥ 23.14 and for all integers x. Theorem 3 is true but needs a new proof. The same false equivalence appears, harmlessly, in 2610.20058. Reviewer: confirmed (moderate). Novelty: v1 only, no public note found. [report](Riemann/Deep/Visser/REPORT.md)
- "Principle of generating critical points of rational functions and Gauss–Fourier conjecture for second-kind zeros" (arXiv 2610.29790, Oct 2026): the necessity direction of Theorem 0.4 is unproved. The step at l. 1691–1694 (repeated at l. 1700–1702) concludes that a root locus meeting the real axis at M from C* comes from a non-real zero and touches the real axis nowhere else, citing Theorems 3.22 and 3.24; but 3.24 is conditional (a meeting with a real-origin locus at b_{i,k}, then departure into C*), its hypotheses and c_{i,k} = M are never verified, and no lemma excludes other real-axis contacts; the definitions of Γ and "directly" (l. 1237–1253) do not supply this. No counterexample to the statement found (108 integer polynomials of degree 3–4). Reviewer: real gap. [audit](Riemann/Tree/R136.lean), [passages](Riemann/Tree/R137.lean)
- "On the sum of least prime factors in short intervals" (arXiv 2608.24930, 2026; Erdős–Graham constant c = 8): the unconditional second-moment claim (abstract item (ii)) rests on an unavailable pointwise bound for primes in intervals of length ≍ (log X)² near X^{1/2} (variance proof, TeX l. 1879–1884); Brun–Titchmarsh does not suffice. A genuine but repairable gap: our reviewer sketches a repair for that range (dyadic decomposition, Selberg's prime-pair upper-bound sieve, Cauchy–Schwarz). The mean result (c = 8) and the conditional result are unaffected. [audit](Riemann/Tree/R161.lean), [review](Riemann/Tree/R164.lean)
- Bellotti, explicit log-free zero-density estimate for ζ (arXiv 2405.12545v2, unpublished): the derivation of the explicit constants is incomplete. The far off-diagonal sum over pairs of zeros counts one representative per distance bin although the rectangles are one-sided (a missing factor about 2 in c₄), and |G(½ + it)| is bounded by a signed coefficient sum, which is not an upper bound (absolute values needed); both feed C₁, C₂ and the tables (correcting c₄ alone raises the final constant by about 15.7% at T₀ = e^500). The theorem is not disproved, but its stated constants are unsupported. Found while auditing a Zenodo proof of Ramanujan's inequality that reuses (4.13). Reviewer: both real flaws. [audit](Riemann/Deep/Ramanujan/REPORT.md), [checks](Riemann/Tree/R162.lean)
- Padhi, "Ramanujan's prime-counting inequality holds for all x ≥ 38,358,837,683" (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.23440770 v2, 2026-10-17; unconditional claim): as published the zero-density step inherits the factor-2 undercount of Bellotti (4.13); corrected, the paper's own certified pipeline exceeds q = 1 near log x = 875 (1.03–1.07 instead of 0.99137), so the claimed range e^58 ≤ x ≤ e^3159 is not proved (a reviewer confirmed the counting objection in principle). liqsweep (14 Oct 2026) claims the result by another route (OpenAI's 7/8 zero-free half-plane); our repair of Padhi's route reaches q ≤ 0.928 but was not completed ([RamRepair](Riemann/Deep/RamRepair/REPORT.md)). [audit](Riemann/Deep/Ramanujan/REPORT.md)
- Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.23555113 (Oct 2026), a claimed proof of RH via Weil positivity: its positivity certificate has the wrong sign. With a = 1.4503086547, c_a = 2^{1−a}√π Γ((1−a)/2)/Γ(a/2) = −5.4587453 (the PDF, p. 8, uses |Γ(−0.225)| and states c_a = 2.34 > 0), so K_a is not positive-definite and W(Φ) ≥ 0 fails. Two independent scouts plus our mpmath check. [R169](Riemann/Tree/R169.lean)
- Bhattacharjee, Bhattacharya, Mandal, Bhattacharya, "Sphere Packing in Dimension 4 and the Twenty-Four-Cell Conjecture" (arXiv 2609.25120 v2, Sep 2026; claims that D₄ is an optimal packing, Δ₄ = π²/16): the shell-localisation lemma (TeX l. 985–1002) and the radial-reduction lemma (l. 1032–1040) are false. Exact packings: a centre at distance 2.9 > 2√2 still cuts the Voronoi cell (volume 1328453/160000 instead of 25/3); a 49-ball packing whose reduced corner has volume 6.594, below the paper's floor 7.799 for contact configurations. So the main theorem is unproved; the missing case is the open 24-cell conjecture. The authors' repository already concedes both lemmas (release v1.4.0, 24 Sep 2026), but arXiv still shows v2 with the claim. [report](Riemann/Deep/D4Pack/REPORT.md)
- Cadeddu, "Proof of the Riemann Hypothesis through the limit of an approximated ξ function" (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.23960915, 4 Nov 2026): breaks at (7). Convergence of the approximant ξ_Q(1−s(Q)) to ξ(1−s) only gives absolute error → 0; at a zero s₀ the denominator ξ(s(Q)) → 0, so the relative error need not tend to 0 (the paper's own (6) says it tends to ∞), and the "contradiction" 0 = ∞ sets (6) against the unproved (7). Nothing uses the Euler product, so the same argument would exclude the off-line zeros of the Davenport–Heilbronn function, which exist. [R210](Riemann/Tree/R210.lean)
- Bonelli, "The Kaleidoscopic Partition Structure, Part II: Resolving the Riemann Hypothesis via the Palindromic Partition Diamond" (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.23788542, 30 Oct 2026, 24k-line PDF): its first closure, Theorem 68.1 (402)–(404), breaks. The Pólya-frequency (PF∞) property of the moments m_s of Φ is derived from "Λ₀(u) = e^{u/2}ϑ₃(0, e^{−πe^{2u}}) ∈ PF∞", which is false: Λ₀ is even and Λ₀(u) ≥ e^{|u|/2}, so it is not even log-concave (PF₂): log Λ₀(0) = 0.0829 while log Λ₀(±1) = 0.5000 (mpmath). Besides, PF∞ of these moments is equivalent to RH (Aissen–Edrei–Schoenberg–Whitney), so the step assumes what it proves. [R214](Riemann/Tree/R214.lean)
- Aksman, "A Heegner-Scattering Program for the Riemann Hypothesis" (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.23775116, 29 Oct 2026): Theorem 6.2, eq. (72), is false. It turns each scattering resonance s_j = 1/2 + iγ_j of the Bianchi orbifold M₁₆₃ into a discrete eigenvalue s_j(L) = s_j + O(L⁻²) of −Δ on a Dehn filling (L² = 1763). With the paper's own normalisation (constant term r^s + φ(s) r^{2−s}, so λ = s(2 − s)), such an eigenvalue has Im λ = γ_j(2 − 2 Re s_j(L)) ≈ γ_j ≠ 0 (γ₁ = 14.13…), impossible for the self-adjoint, nonnegative −Δ of a closed orbifold; no perturbation theorem is given. [R216](Riemann/Tree/R216.lean)
- Aksman, "Non-Hermitian Cusp Dissipation, Automorphic Scattering, and an Unconditional Geometric Proof of the Riemann Hypothesis" (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.23646708, 25 Oct 2026): the closing Rouché argument (93)–(95), p. 13, lets the cutoff height Y → 0⁺, but the pseudo-Laplacian Δ̃_Y and Lemma 8.2 (all zeros of Φ_Y on Re s = 1) are defined only for Y ≥ 1 (Definition 8.1). For admissible Y ≥ 1 and Re s ≥ σ₀ − ε > 1, |Y^{2s−2}| = Y^{2 Re s − 2} ≥ 1, so (95) would need inf |φ_K| > 1 on the circle, which is not shown; the contradiction is not reached.
