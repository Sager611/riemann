- Lemma A (subfields of CM fields). E CM with involution ι (σ∘ι = conj∘σ for every σ : E → ℂ), L ⊂ E. If σ, σ′ agree on L then σ(ιx) = conj σ(x) = σ′(ιx) for x ∈ L, so ι(L) = L; hence ι|_L is complex conjugation for every embedding of L: L is totally real (ι|_L = 1) or CM. K₃ is neither, so no CM field contains K₃ or H ⊇ K₃.
- Lemma B (which Hecke characters pass the host step). ψ of finite order on an imaginary quadratic F, field L_ψ. If L_ψ lies in a CM field, complex conjugation c is central in Gal(L̃/ℚ), so ψ^c = ψ; then ψ extends to G_ℚ (invariant character, cyclic quotient), i.e. ψ = χ∘N_{F/ℚ} with χ Dirichlet (Kronecker–Weber) and L(s,ψ) = L(s,χ)L(s,χχ_F).
