### 8.5 Smallest test cases and what a counterexample would be
- a = 1 (both statements): U(1) Hecke characters. CongFinOdd's type step is elementary Fourier analysis: with B = Z/l^m the image of J, −Σ_{ψ≠1} θ_Uψ̃ = θ_U(1 − l^m δ_0), error l^{−m} → 0 (coefficients −1). PlanEx for a = 1 is CM theory. Both should hold; failure would be a bug in definitions.
- First genuine case: a = 3. Apart w u := w and u lie over different places of F⁺ (Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Statements/EllipticCurveLevels/ClosedAtLConclusionOfMem.lean:16-17), so w₀, v' need two places of F⁺ above l: imaginary quadratic E is excluded; smallest: a quartic CM field, e.g. E = Q(ζ_8) (F⁺ = Q(√2)) with l ≡ 1 mod 8 (l = 17), definite U(3) over F⁺ of small tame level.
  - CongFinOdd counterexample: a Hecke eigensystem θ of a definite U(3) form (e.g. an Eisenstein or endoscopic one, Rogawski's packets from U(1)×U(2)) that is not in the PolyApprox closure of eigensystems with a wild supercuspidal type at w₀ and lacunary weight at v' at the same tame level. Decisive form: a finite tame level and ε such that, for every depth and weight, sup_P |P(θ) − Σ c_η P(η)| > ε; Emerton–Paškūnas-type hypotheses (residual irreducibility, l large) mark where to look.
  - PlanEx counterexample: a definite U(3) form with a supercuspidal type at the split w₀ whose eigensystem twisted by any algebraic κ occurs on no compact Picard surface (division algebra of degree 3 over E with involution of the second kind, signature (1, 2)) at any level N. A supercuspidal component at a split place excludes endoscopy (the base change to GL_3 is cuspidal), so the classical transfer predicts no counterexample; a failure would refute Jacquet–Langlands/Clozel, i.e. point to a defect in SpecTop or Vec.
