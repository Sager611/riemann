# Artin half-plane deep dive: report (2026-10-20)
Full proofs: [NOTE.md](NOTE.md). Every result is conditional on the claimed 2026 theorems: OpenAI 7/8 (Dirichlet) and Liu B♭ (all Hecke). These are Lean-supported but not refereed.

## Brief items
- (a) Artin holomorphy in Re s > B♭: TRUE, KNOWN. L(s,ρ) is holomorphic and zero-free there, with a pole of order ⟨ρ,1⟩ at s = 1. All Artin poles therefore lie in 1−B♭ ≤ Re s ≤ B♭.
  Hu (Hexagon 2610.00216, 13 Oct, Cor 9.4) proves it at 7/8 by Brauer induction. Liu's Corollary (2) covers only monomial ρ. B♭ is a substitution. Proof: NOTE §3.1.
- (b) Power-saving Chebotarev: TRUE, KNOWN. Frei–Sofos–Widmer (arXiv 2610.18491, Thm 1.1) prove the bound c(|C|/|G|)x^{1−δ}δ^{−1}log(Δ_L x^{n_L}) under the hypothesis that ζ_L has no zeros with Re s > 1−δ. Hu Cor 9.6 and Cojocaru–Meyer 2023 Thm 4 also have it. With Liu the error is x^{B♭}.
- (c) Least Chebotarev prime: TRUE, KNOWN. FSW Cor 1.2 gives (log Δ_L)^{1/δ}(log log Δ_L)^{2/δ}. Hu (9.10) gives (log eΔ_L+n_L)^8 with no log log factor. With Liu: N𝔭 ≤ (C log 3Δ_L)^{7.9928}, a substitution. Smooth proof: NOTE §3.3.
- (d) NEW, not found in any source:
  - **D.** P(a,q) ≪_η q^{7/3+η}, uniform in q; effective if Chen–Gupta–Li's constants are (their paper never states effectivity; its proof has no Siegel-zero input). The 12/5 version from Huxley is effective (NOTE Prop 2, Thm D). Previous records: 5 (Xylouris 2011) and 3.99 (Naslund, Hexagon 2610.00018, Oct 2026, without QRH). Under GRH: 2.
  - **D'.** ψ(x;q,a) = (x/φ(q))(1+O(x^{−c})) for q ≤ x^{3/7−ε}; with Huxley alone, q ≤ x^{5/12−ε}. Before, the range was q ≤ (log x)^A (Siegel–Walfisz), or q ≤ x^{1/8} from 7/8 alone.
    D' is also CGL's Cor 1.4, whose zero-free-region hypothesis OpenAI's 7/8 now satisfies.
  - **E (minor).** Every ray class group Cl_𝔪(K) is generated by degree-one primes of norm ≤ (C log 3Δ_K N𝔪)^{7.9928}. Bach's bound under GRH is 12(log Δ_K)². Proof: NOTE §4.1.
  - **F (minor).** Res_{s=1}ζ_K ≥ e^{−81}/log Δ_K for every K ≠ ℚ, with an absolute effective constant. This makes Brauer–Siegel effective for every family with n/log Δ → 0, with no Galois hypothesis. Proof: NOTE §4.2.
