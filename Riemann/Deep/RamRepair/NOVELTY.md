- Their proof: Theorem B (if ζ(s) ≠ 0 for Re s > θ with θ ≤ 0.945092, then N_R = 38,358,837,683; Cully-Hugill–Johnston explicit formula, Büthe's method with RH to 3·10^12, interval arithmetic) plus OpenAI's 7/8 zero-free half-plane (Lean-checked; our port Riemann/Deep/QRH builds sorry-free). Not refereed. Its only unrefereed inputs are the 7/8 theorem and Bhattacharjee's 2024 MSc correction of Büthe 2016 Thm 1. Dated 14 Oct, so before Padhi (Zenodo v1, 16 Oct) and before us (22 Oct).
- What is still ours: a proof that does not use the 7/8 half-plane. Without it, liqsweep leaves (e^753.98, e^3035.01) open: Thm A covers N_R ≤ x ≤ e^753.98 (Chirre–Helfgott preprint; e^672.84 with refereed inputs only), Thm C covers x ≥ e^3035.01 (published inputs) and C* covers x ≥ e^2591.01 (BTY and Bellotti preprints). So ours is a second proof of a known statement, one that avoids the half-plane; it is not a new theorem.
