## Transfers, tests, verdicts
**T1 (N13) Duvall/Abel star.** (i) Transfer: the family of Dirichlet L-functions obeys Duvall's law exactly, w = q·γ, F(w) = ½ ln(w/2πe); Gough's Abel inversion gives the closed form c(r) = 2r ln(R/r) (forward check: F = ½ ln(2w/(e a_s)) to 10⁻⁴ at w ≤ 100), a single sequence gives the cutoff profile τ(ω_c) = (1/π)[(π/2 − φ0) ln(ω_c/2π) + ∫_{φ0}^{π/2} ln sin], sin φ0 = 2π/ω_c (forward WKB reproduces N̄ to 2·10⁻⁴ relative; ω_c ≈ 4πe^{2τ}). (ii) Test: zeros of ζ and of the 9 real primitive characters of conductor 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 8′, 11, 12, 13 (138–259 zeros each, to 300) fall on one Duvall curve, residual rms 0.0033–0.0045 for γ > 150 (= πS/γ, the glitches). Physical-star properties hold trivially (c > 0, c/r monotone, τ(ω_c) monotone) except a bounded acoustic cutoff (the zeta star traps infinitely many modes); none touches zeros. (iii) DH: the full list (with its 9 pairs as double ordinates) lies on the conductor-5 curve (rms 0.0031); the sign-change list drifts by −2 per pair (−2.1, −8.1, −10.1, −17.8 at 100, 200, 300, 419): that is Turing's method. mv is ζ's star. Restatement: Riemann–von Mangoldt with analytic conductor (Iwaniec–Sarnak), Wu–Sprung, Turing. Verdict: known, DH-blind.

**T2 (N8/N10/N2) Glitch analysis of zero ordinates.** (i) Transfer: prime powers are acoustic glitches at depth ½ ln n. Window [2000, 20000] (20,974 zeros, L̄ = 7.433): G(ln n) = Σw n^{iγ}/Σw equals −Λ(n)/(√n L̄) to 0.2 % for every prime power n ≤ 40 and is 0 (< 10⁻⁵) at the other n ≤ 40; it stays exact far above the "Nyquist" prime √(T1/2π) = 17.8 (p = 101, 499, 997 to 0.2 %) and degrades only past p ≈ T2/2π = 3183 (2.8 % at 4999, lost at 10007), i.e. when the glitch leaves the cavity; background at generic λ 1.3·10⁻⁷. The asteroseismic depth/radius alias has no analogue: the dual sum C_m = Σw e^{2iθ(γ)} m^{−iγ}/Σw equals −1/(√m L̄) to 0.6 % for every m ≤ 16 (C_6 = −0.0551 vs −0.0549), not the alias value −Λ(m)/(√m L̄) (0 at m = 6). (ii) Detector test on DH (window [20, 420]): its spectrum has non-prime-power glitches (b(6) = (1 + κ²) ln 6 = 1.936, b(4) = −1.442, b(9) = −2.286, coefficients of −f′/f), matched within 1.5 % by the sum over its complex zeros (crude normalisation by L̄); at generic λ the residual against the prime side is 3.7·10⁻⁶ for the complex zeros, 3.47·10⁻³ for the ordinates (pairs doubled) — exactly the predicted 2(cosh λδ − 1) term (remainder 2.7·10⁻⁵) — and 2.3·10⁻² for the sign-change list. (iii) Adversarial: any statistic of ordinates alone is identical for an off-line pair, an on-line double zero and (to O(gap)) a Lehmer pair: ζ's smallest normalised gaps are 0.042 (n = 6709), 0.044 (18859), 0.046 (4765), a moved pair gives 0; δ enters only through the prime side, at second order (λ²δ²), which is Booker's explicit-formula zero verification, and amplitude growth with depth is von Koch's criterion. Second-difference/gap statistics flag near-collisions, i.e. Lehmer pairs and Csordas–Smith–Varga lower bounds for Λ (superseded by Λ ≥ 0). Verdict: known; not a certificate.

**T3 (N1) SOLA/OLA kernels in Weil's form.** (i) Transfer: a positive band-limited averaging kernel A = F·F* is exactly a Weil test function, and an off-line pair acts as an on-line double zero plus a curvature charge −2δ²A″(γ̄) (+O(δ⁴)). (ii) Test (mv, L = 1 … 4, Backus–Gilbert spread-optimal taper cos(πx/L) and node kernels): charge = −2δ²A″ to < 1 % when δL ≤ 0.3, ≤ 2.3 % at δ = 0.15 and ≤ 9 % at δ = 0.3 (the O(δ⁴L⁴) term); SOLA kernels centred on γ̄ register an excess (+0.0004 … +0.46), tails and node kernels a deficit (down to −0.61), while Q = 11 … 227 never approaches 0. (iii) DH and mv satisfy every positive-kernel inequality away from the null-steering regime; making Q negative needs kernels convex at γ̄ and nearly null at all real zeros, the window problem of explore19 (a merged pair is first seen at c* ≈ 0.072m + 0.73); Bernstein bounds the charge by 2δ²L² sup A. Restatement: Weil's criterion (Taylor expansion of A at γ̄ ∓ iδ) and Slepian concentration. Verdict: known.

**T4 (N13/N12) Mixed modes and avoided crossings.** DH on the critical line is u(Z(t) + Z(−t)), Z(t) = ε^{1/2}Λ(1/2 + it, χ̄) real (checked: ratio 0.51978, spread 6·10⁻¹¹): the Hardy functions of χ and χ̄ added with no coupling, so no avoided crossings. Verdict: no route.
