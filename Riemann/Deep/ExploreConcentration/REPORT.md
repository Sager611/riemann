## Statements
- **S1 (T^∞, proved).** σ > ½: log|ζ_lift(σ, θ)| = −Σ_p log|1 − p^{−σ}e^{iθ_p}| has mean 0 and is L_re-Lipschitz in ℓ², L_re(σ)² = Σ_p p^{−2σ}(1 − p^{−2σ})^{−2} (complex log: L_arg² = Σ_p p^{−2σ}(1 − p^{−σ})^{−2}). Herbst: P(±log|ζ_lift| ≥ r) ≤ e^{−r²/(2L_re²)}; the Bohr–Jessen law of log ζ(σ+it) satisfies T₂(L_arg²). DH (F = c₁L(s,χ) + c̄₁L(s,χ̄), χ mod 5, c₁ = (1 − ik)/2, k = 0.2840): its lift vanishes on T^∞, log|F_lift| is not Lipschitz, lower tail ≍ e^{−2r}.
- **S2 (Littlewood).** F = 1 + Σ_{n≥2} a_n n^{−s}: (2π/T)Σ_{β>σ, T<γ≤2T}(β − σ) = E_T log|F(σ+it)| + O(log T/T). On T^∞, E log|ζ_lift| = 0 and E log|DH_lift| = φ_DH(σ) > 0 (Jessen function). The zero sum is the mean shift of the non-Lipschitz function log|·|; LSI/Herbst bound fluctuations about means, never this shift.
- **Prop TL (transport form of Littlewood's lemma).** μ_T = law of ζ(σ+it), t uniform on [T, 2T]; μ_∞ = Bohr–Jessen law; R > 0:
  (2π/T)Σ_{β>σ}(β − σ) ≤ e^R·W₁(μ_T, μ_∞) + A(R) + O(log T/T),  A(R) = E log⁺(e^{−R}/|ζ_lift|) ≤ √(πL_re²/2)·erfc(R/√(2L_re²)).
  Proof: log|z| ≤ log max(|z|, e^{−R}), which is e^R-Lipschitz; Kantorovich–Rubinstein; E log|ζ_lift| = 0; layer cake + Herbst for A. Hence zero sum ≤ W₁^{1−o(1)}. Converse, with g = (e₀ − |z|)⁺: W₁ ≥ (e₀²/8M)·#{ρ : |β − σ| ≤ e₀/8M}/(T·m_loc) − e₀μ_∞(B(0, e₀)) (M a common bound for |ζ′| on the discs, m_loc the maximal overlap with multiplicity, intervals clipped to [T, 2T]), and μ_∞(B(0, ε)) = o(ε^A). So TL bounds the zero sum by W₁, and conversely any W₁ rate bounds the zero count near the line Re s = σ: complementary one-sided inequalities (not an equivalence of the two quantities), which make a W₁ rate at least as strong as a near-line density bound; no gain. For DH the same proof gives only zero sum ≤ φ_DH + e^R W₁ + A_DH(R), A_DH ≍ e^{−2R}.
- **S3 (where it breaks).** (a) Tail route: a Gaussian lower tail on [T, 2T] up to level r*, for all σ ≥ σ₀, gives N(σ₀ + e^{−r*}/(4M), T) ≤ 8M²(1 − σ₀)T·exp(2r* − r*²/(2L²)) (strong once r* > 4L² = 11.1 at σ₀ = 0.6; zero-free if r* = ∞), and each zero within e^{−r}/(4M) of the line adds e^{−r}/(2MT) to that tail: the [T, 2T] tail is itself a density statement near σ₀. It does not transfer for free: Selberg's log ζ ≈ P_X holds off an exceptional set whose measure is the density input, and k matched moments of P_X (X^k ≤ T) give only polynomial tails (CkL²/r²)^k. Output = input up to e^{2r}: circular. (b) Jensen route: E_T log|ζM| ≤ ½ log E_T|ζM|² loses exactly Var log|ζM|; replacing Jensen by Herbst needs E_T|ζM|^{2k}, the Ingham/Montgomery mean values. (c) Weissler–Bayart: ‖F(·+s)‖_{H^q} ≤ ‖F‖_{H²} iff 2^{−s} ≤ √(2/q), so s = ½log₂(q/2) ≥ ½ for q ≥ 4: off the strip beyond the 4th moment; removing p < p_min gives s = log(q/2)/(2 log p_min) at cost Π_{p<p_min}(1 − p^{−½−s})^{−1} (q = 8, p_min = 10007: s = 0.075, cost 2.2·10⁸); on [T, 2T] the inequality for ζ^k is Titchmarsh 13.2's mean-value theorem (Lindelöf in mean).
- **N8 side (Lemma M of Theorem U′).** DPC's R_T is a degree-≤ k chaos on T^∞; Weissler gives E_∞|R_T|^q ≤ (q/2)^{kq/2}‖R_T‖₂^q for every q and θ (R = P_rQ, r = √(2/q)). ## Adversarial
DH: S1 fails for it (lift zeros, e^{−2R} small-ball term, Jessen mean 0.044 > 0), so the Lipschitz/Herbst property does separate ζ from DH, but only as Bohr–Jessen's "the lift of ζ has no zeros", i.e. Bohr–Landau o(T); at every accessible level DH satisfies Herbst's bound too, and DH's [T, 2T] statistics match its own lift (table). GUE/Poisson zero models: no Bohr lift, so S1/TL do not apply; for Lemma M a GUE or Poisson zero set on the line gives W = 0 and all moments, i.e. behaves like RH. In the transfers tested, nothing in the tools distinguishes zero locations; the Euler-product information they bring to [T, 2T] is the lift's zero-freeness (its small-ball bound) plus prime independence on T^∞, which only gives pseudo-moments.

Not examined (scope): multiscale prime-block methods (Soundararajan, Harper under RH; Heap–Radziwiłł–Soundararajan, Arguin–Bailey unconditionally). They are N9's state of the art and use prime-block independence on T^∞ with mean values of short block polynomials plus RH or mollifier twisted moments; LSI/hypercontractivity could replace their Taylor-truncation step, which changes constants, not the length budget or the RH input. Untested here, so the barrier is scoped to the five transfers above.
