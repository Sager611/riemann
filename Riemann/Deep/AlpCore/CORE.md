## 8. Human-readable sketch (what the Lean argues, bottom-up; [C] = classical, [N] = new/unexplained)
Fix a primitive χ (PChar ψ). Choose ε ∈ (0, 1/52) and large x. Build an abelian 'host': a 2-power cyclotomic layer of degree 2^e ≍ x^(2+8ε) (PageN), cut by ψ, and two auxiliary primes q ≡ 3 (8), d ≡ 7 (16), log d ≍ Y^(ε/5)log²Y, every small odd prime dividing d+1 (WinPair) — so K = Q(√−d) is the CM field and all primes ≤ Y^(ε/5) split in K. Group: E0 × H, H = Z/n, Phi = {g : g.1.1 = 0} = Frobenii of primes split in K (ArithData, HostFacts).
1. Analytic endgame [specialised Landau theorem; Lean-proved; classical only in spirit (Landau, Turán-type localisation)]. If a positive combination Gm = Gz + Σ wt·Re Gchi (Riesz-smoothed, ExplicitFormulaErrorOfHostConditions.lean:39) satisfies Gm(y) ≤ C·y^θ on windows [Y, Y^(1+δ)] for arbitrarily large Y, every L(s,ψ) with ψ in the level is zero-free for Re s > θ (Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/ZeroFreeOfRpowWindow.lean:11 via lemmaL; file names IteratedDerivLaplace, LandauKernelMax suggest localisation by high derivatives of the Laplace transform; la = 3/2). Real zeros are caught because 1/(β(β−1)) < 0 makes their Riesz contribution positive. Then θ ↓ 1/2 (TopWin.lean:58 level_of_tiedWin), imprimitive characters by Euler factors (CharacterBoxes/EveryCharacter.lean plugAll).
2. Orthogonality [C]. The one-sided bound is an UPPER bound for ASK, a weighted Riesz sum Σ 2 log p (a/p^i − w)^+ over INERT prime powers in prescribed classes: ASK − MAIN = De (colour_balance, StronglyIrreducible.lean:695), MAIN = a(log a − 1 − γ + L'/L(1,χ_K) − mean log w). Test C: on real primes ASK − MAIN stays at √y scale through 2·10⁶. Caveat: ASK − MAIN is a SIGNED combination (−Gchi for χ_K and other characters); §9 explains how the Lean reduces it to the positive Gm (host conditions on the 'Bounded' characters).
3. Integrality ⇒ upper bound (Chebyshev's binomial idea, Stepanov's shape) [N]. One builds hermitian lattices (DivData: RR, Nu u, maps Bk u : Nu u → RR^∨ with integral image, heights h u) from theta lifts. Inert primes divide the relevant periods: at a supersingular (inert) prime p the a-th invariant derivative is divisible by p^(a/p + a/p³ + … − O(log a)) [classical phenomenon: height-2 formal group, cf. Hurwitz-number integrality; Test A checks the exponent on Z[i], Z[ω] for a ≤ 2000; the Lean's precise normalisation (MramL/NuDef, DataDIVDefs.lean:12-55) not compared], and only odd powers count, matching 'i·frob p ∉ Phi' in ASK. This divisibility raises the slope of Nu u by Ga ≈ ASK (SlopesN, Losses). A Bost-type slope inequality (SlopeIneq) [C-type] bounds slopes of Nu by the slope of RR^∨ and the heights; DIV bounds that slope by the ARCHIMEDEAN size (exact main expression plus an explicitly bounded remainder; needs nonvanishing/injectivity of the evaluation maps and the integrality OneMapIntoCC, DataArith.lean:139-182): Σ_t m·(ph(a_t) − a_t·logOm) − Σ_t a_t log T + errors, ph(y) = y log y − y (Stirling), logOm = γ + log(π/v) − L'/L(1,χ_K) (Chowla–Selberg period, ModuliProofs/PrincipalRootReZeroImSq.lean:25-60). The order-m main terms cancel against Main (remark item 9) and MeanBoundSecondForm gives mean ASK ≤ mean Main + ErrX.
4. Multiplicity from a chain of theta lifts [N; Rallis inner product and seesaw identities themselves are classical, their integral, nonvanishing, uniformly controlled m-fold iteration is the new claim]. A chain U(X_0) → … → U(X_m) of theta lifts, X_j of dimension N(j) = k + j(t+1) (Params.lean:36), paired with spaces W_j of constant dimension r = k+m−1 and signature sigW j = (k+j−1, m−j) (only W_m definite, FactWmDef), each step twisting by Hecke characters μ_j, η (Com, ChainParSucc.lean:19-36), with exact Rallis-type inner products and positive scalars per step (DecoratedLatticeProofs/ThetaLiftChain*Statement.lean) and a seesaw identity (DoubleLiftFockSumEqSumIteratedLifts), gives m lifts, each contributing its own inert-prime divisibility (Data.ASK u0 = Σ_t Σ_{j<m} ASK(a_t, t, w_{u0,j}): m copies, like vanishing to order m) against the factor m·ph(a_t) on the archimedean side of DIV, while each lift costs only an archimedean Γ-sum kerPlace (DataDIVDefs.lean:74, Harish-Chandra parameters of the interlacing (GGP) type HCParam, Params.lean:48-70).
5. Bookkeeping [N, Lean-checked]. Error/Main ≍ 1/m (single-lift error) + m/x (per-lift cost) ⇒ m ≍ √x (RestSize: x^(1/2−ε) ≤ m; SizesTextAt m ≍ x^(1/2−ε/16)), giving Gm ≤ x^(1/2+O(ε)) (SChain.lean ChainAt). A per-lift cost growing like m would give m ≍ x^(1/3) and exponent 2/3 (Test B; remark item 14).
6. Arithmetic input VWin [N, deep]. 'Weak Vandiver': Σ_l log l Σ_{e<x^401} length Ш¹(G_{E,S}, Q_l/Z_l(−e)) ≤ exp(x^ε/2) for the host fields E (ExistsPageN.lean:12-49; Formalism/GaloisCohomology/Statement.lean:54-78, LData pinned to the cyclotomic character, not vacuous by definition). It feeds HypV/TopG (ZeroFreeAllOfTopG.lean:12-26), i.e. the existence of families on which the chain inequalities hold. Proved via vwin_of_row (VwinOfRow.lean:4); not in the literature. Remark fn 6 says K-group vanishing for Z follows; the displayed bound exp(x^ε/2) grows with x, so that would need a further argument (not traced).
Euler product: used essentially — primVal is multiplicative (FirstPrimeNeSecondPrime.lean:65), the argument is per-prime divisibility at inert primes (DataDIVDefs.lean:45-55 InertK/NuDef), and Frobenius classes come from a real abelian extension (HostFacts; TieArith ties primVal to Dirichlet characters, TieGlobal.lean). A Davenport–Heilbronn function has none of these, so the method cannot apply to it — no inconsistency.

## 9. Sign bridge (raised by the reviewer, logs/reviewer_core.md): how a signed combination becomes the positive Gm
- The thick part of ASK − MAIN averages over translates u0 ∈ H to Gsig = G(0) + G(psC) − G(chC) − G(chpsC) (Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/GrowthBoundGivesRiemannHypothesis.lean:33-49, Gsig, ThickPartTranslateMean); chC(g) = (−1)^(g.1.1) is the character of the CM field K = Q(√−d) (FirstPrimeNeSecondPrime.lean:21). So the core inequality alone bounds a SIGNED combination; a real zero of L(s,χ_K) would enter with the wrong sign (Test C's Gz − Gchi is the one-character case).
- The thin part (weights w = b' tiny, arguments a/w ≥ X'/3 = exp(x^(ε/3))/3) is killed by ThinScales: ‖GPsi ψ y‖ ≤ y·θ, θ = exp(−x^(ε/8)) (same file :55; host form Analytic.lean ChebyshevCharacterBound). Exponents are consistent with the classical zero-free region: log(conductor) ≲ x^(ε/5)log²x and 2/15 > 1/8.
- The final route does not need Gz alone: it bounds Gm = Gz + Σ_{KEPT∖1} wt·Re Gchi with KEPT = ⟨ψ⟩ (Level.ofSub/Level.cyc, ExplicitFormulaErrorOfHostConditions.lean:105-121) and requires of each host (EAtH, :144-150): KeptInXp, ExceptionalCharacterKept, Shares, BoundedCharsPointwise ‖G(θ,x)‖ ≤ x^(1/2+ε) at the single point x for every 'Bounded' θ = (Xp ∖ Kept) ∪ Xm (Analytic.lean:90-136; Xp/Xm = characters trivial on the first factor with θ(iod) = ±1), and |L'/L(1,χ)| ≤ C log x. Host existence on windows: HostExW (3/2) (GChi ψ).req (GlobalTwisting/ThinScalesIffHostBound.lean:120; CharacterBoxes/Supply.lean:42 GChi = EAtH ∧ VVle), supplied by supplyChi (CharacterBoxes/SupplyChi.lean:8) from VWinAt via supplyOfAdm and admissibleClassesTwistedChar; mean-square variant BoundedCharsMeanSquare (AutomorphicArithmetic/QuadraticCharacters/ExceptionalCharacterCondition.lean:41-46).
- Assessment: a GRH-size bound at x for every Bounded character is not classical for a fixed family. It is plausible only if every Bounded character carries an auxiliary quadratic character of q or d (conductor ≫ x^(O(1/ε)); high moments over the choice of d give it); which factor is the 'first factor' was NOT traced. This is the least-traced load-bearing step after VWin.
