NEW: no source states the classification of the boundary line n odd, m+k = n−1 (Theorem A's criterion, the orbit formula, G ≅ D_{2α}≀D_{2n/α} when (k+1) | m, primitive and ⊇ A_n otherwise). Checked 2026-10-23. The known pieces below must be credited in NOTE.md.
Known 1, off the line and n even: 2610.30892 Thm 3.1. The same result was proved earlier in Lean in GitHub the-omega-institute/trureturing, files D5/S0/CayleyGrowth/PrefixReversalTriple{Odd,Even}NonGeneration.lean, 2026-09-13. Neither source treats the odd line.
Known 2, gcd(m+1,k+1) = 1 is necessary: 2610.30892, Lemma at TeX l. 277. It says that if any triple generates S_n then gcd(n−m, n−k) = 1, and on the line n−m = k+1 and n−k = m+1. Its special case n−m | n+1 is 2511.16959 Prop. 2. NOTE.md does not credit this lemma yet.
Known 3, the n-cycle: in the proof of Thm 1 (TeX l. 484), 2511.16959 already uses the n-cycle r_n r_{n−3} r_2, which is conjugate to our P when k = 2. Not found anywhere: the general first-return description of P, the Beatty-set coordinates, the block analysis and the D≀D structure.
Known 4, k = 2 and k = 3 on the line: 2511.16959 Thms 1 and 3, including the blocks of 3 for (n, n−3, 2) when 3 | n.
Known 5, A_n versus S_n: this is the parity observation of Bass–Sudborough (JPDC 2003, Obs. 2.2(4)).
Wording of Conjecture 4: v1 (2025-11-21) and v2 (2026-09-01) of 2511.16959 state the parity-split form. Only v3, which is the published DMTCS 28:1 text (dmtcs:16975), says "if m+k < n". So the counterexamples on the line hit a wording slip in v3, not the authors' conjecture, and are not a discovery.
2610.30892 also proves Conjectures 2–3: shifting all three indices by 4, or by 2 when n ≡ 0,1 mod 4, preserves generation. A shift never moves a generating triple onto the line, so nothing here follows from it. Minor: both shift proofs write "m+k−1 ≥ n" where m+k ≥ n−1 is meant. This is harmless because t ≥ 3 (resp. t ≥ 1) still holds.
