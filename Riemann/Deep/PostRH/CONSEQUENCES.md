## The five statements (.scratch/alp/lean/src/Main.lean l.4–12, Statements.lean l.3–16)
- L1 `riemannHypothesis : RiemannHypothesis` (Mathlib) and L2 `RiemannZetaZerosOnLine` (Statements l.3–4): ζ(s) = 0, s ∉ {−2, −4, …}, s ≠ 1 ⟹ Re s = 1/2.
- L3 `DirichletLNonvanishingRightOfHalf` (l.6–8): every N ≥ 1, every χ mod N (imprimitive and principal included), Re s > 1/2, (χ ≠ 1 ∨ s ≠ 1) ⟹ L(χ, s) ≠ 0.
- L4 `DirichletLZerosOnLine` (l.10–12): 0 < Re s < 1, L(χ, s) = 0 ⟹ Re s = 1/2, every χ mod N. L5 (l.14–16): the same for primitive χ.
- Derived (routine): D1 GRH for every Dirichlet L (nontrivial zeros of primitive L lie in 0 < Re s < 1; imprimitive Euler factors vanish only on Re s = 0). D2 GRH for ζ_K of every abelian K/Q (ζ_K = ∏ L(s, χ*) over the primitive characters of K, conductor–discriminant formula; used in COROLLARIES.md l.46). D3 GLH for ζ and every Dirichlet L ("GRH implies the Generalized Lindelöf Hypothesis", LLS arXiv 1309.3595 l.232). D4 no Siegel zero for any Dirichlet L, effective constants.
- NOT given: GRH for ζ_K of non-abelian or non-Galois K; Hecke L of K ≠ Q beyond products of Dirichlet L (e.g. non-genus class-group characters, Grössencharacters of infinite order); non-abelian Artin L; GL(2), elliptic-curve, symmetric-power, Maass, automorphic L; the Selberg class; and extra hypotheses (simple zeros, pair correlation, LI, moment conjectures).

- N2 de Bruijn–Newman. Λ = 0: Λ ≥ 0 (Rodgers–Tao arXiv 1801.05914 Theorem main, l.129: "One has Λ ≥ 0") and RH ⟺ Λ ≤ 0 (l.68).
