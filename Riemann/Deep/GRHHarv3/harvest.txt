   Lee and O'Clarey recently conjectured that every integer $n>24$ can be written as the sum of a prime and a positive integer that is not square-free. They proved this for odd $n$, and for all $n>24$ assuming the generalised Riemann hypothesis for Dirichlet $L$-functions.
