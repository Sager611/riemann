# Kissing number in dimension 38: tau_38 >= 624 020 (was 611 522)

## Statement
There is a kissing configuration of **624 020** points in R^38. Previous best: **611 522**
(A. Kravatskiy, github.com/alexlegeartis/KissingNumbers, dim38-joint-latitudes, 28 Oct 2026; listed in
Cohn's table https://cohn.mit.edu/kissing-numbers/ on 4 Nov 2026). Gain **+12 498**.

## Construction (one paragraph)
Kravatskiy's 611 522-point configuration in R^24 + R^14 = 589 560 caps (+-u, sqrt2 w)/sqrt48 (u a Leech
minimal vector) + 1 596 axis points (0, y) + 6 656 latitude points (body of squared length 1/4) + an **equator**:
Q. Lin's 6 855 lines q of squared norm 48 in the same Leech lattice L, pairwise |q.q'| <= 24, giving 13 710
points (+-q, 0)/sqrt48. Any such set of norm-48 lines may serve as the equator (Yang et al. 2609.35051,
Lemma 7.1): against a cap the cosine is q.u/48 <= 1/2 because q -+ u != 0 lies in L (min norm 32), against a
latitude point Cauchy-Schwarz gives <= 1/2, axis points are orthogonal. Lin found 6 855 lines by local search
(independent set in a 11178-regular graph on 8 386 560 lines, alpha known only in [6855, 98280]).
**New:** take the norm-48 vectors of the sublattice

    M = { x in L : F x = 0 (mod 3) }       (F = the 6 x 24 matrix in F3.txt, index [L:M] = 3^6)

M has no vector of norm 32, so for two of its norm-48 vectors v != +-w both v - w and v + w are nonzero in M,
hence of norm >= 48, hence |v.w| <= 24. M has exactly **26 208** vectors of norm 48 = **13 104 lines**
(equator_lines_13104.txt). Replacing Lin's equator: 611 522 - 13 710 + 26 208 = **624 020**.
(Coordinates: L = {x in Z^24 : common parity e, ((x-e)/2 mod 2) in the Golay code of golay_basis.txt,
sum x = 4e mod 8}, min norm 32; the same file and coordinates as Lin's and Kravatskiy's packages.)

## How M was found (method)
1. Eisenstein structure: random words in Conway's xi_T = eps_T eta_T and sign changes generate Co_0 elements;
   a power of one is a fixed-point-free automorphism omega of order 3 (omega8.txt = 8*omega), omega^2+omega+1=0.
2. theta = omega - omega^2 (theta^2 = -3); L/theta L = F_3^12. The 196 560 minimal vectors give 65 520 classes T.
3. The Fourier transform of T on F_3^12 is two-valued off 0: **-90** (465 920 times), **639** (65 520 times).
   Hence a subspace C avoiding T has |C^perp| >= 1 + 65520/90 = 729, i.e. dim C <= 6, with equality iff C^perp \ 0
   lies in the -90 set. A greedy random search finds 6-dim C instantly (6 independent C tried: all give 13 104 lines).
4. M = preimage of C (contains theta L). Its 26 208 norm-6 (std scale) vectors = the q^3 coefficient of the
   extremal 3-modular theta series in dim 24 (1 + 26208 q^3 + 530712 q^4 + ...). Not checked: whether M is
   3-modular (theta M* is not contained in M for our theta).
Scripts: discover.py (NumPy, ~10 s, re-finds omega, the two Fourier values, a C and F), construct.py (NumPy, ~15 s,
regenerates the 13 104 lines from F3.txt and compares).
