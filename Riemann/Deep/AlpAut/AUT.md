- Fin0 (:172-175): for every IsPt lift ρ to a complete local O-algebra R, O[mono ρ 0] is a finite O-module.

## 3. 'Automorphic' (AV/ModularityArgument/AutomorphyLifting/IsAutCsdA.lean:16-21)
- IsAut4Alt ι r := IsAutQA ι r.toHomUnits (DTC/IsAut4.lean:13). IsAutQA ι r: r continuous ⇒ for EVERY CM field K with [K:Q] = 2 (imaginary quadratic) such that r|G_K is irreducible: ∃ π ∈ CuspSub K 4 regular algebraic (IsRegAlg) with AttC ι (r|G_K) π, and WitA ι (r|G_K). This is automorphy of the restrictions r|G_K to imaginary quadratic K (where irreducible), NOT automorphy over Q (equivalent only via quadratic descent under extra hypotheses). AttC ι ρ π (AV/AutomorphicForms/BlochKatoSelmerVanishes.lean:69-83): ρ semisimple and, at cofinitely many places w, ρ unramified with charpoly(ρ(Frob_w⁻¹)) = ∏(X − q_w^((n−1)/2)·α) over a Satake parameter of π (HasSat). CuspSub K n (Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/AutomorphicMembership/ExpoConjOfIsCsd.lean:209-216): nonzero irreducible stable subspace of cusp forms; IsRegAlg (:591-593): regular algebraic infinity type.
- IsAut4 (AV/AutomorphicCases/SymplecticGSp4.lean:22) := Af.IsAutQ ι r.toHomUnits; IsAutQ and IsAutQA have the same type (IsAutCsdA.lean:23); Sp4In (:24-27) is the IsAut4 twin of SymplecticFourImageDense.
