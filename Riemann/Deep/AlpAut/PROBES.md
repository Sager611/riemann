## P1. LemmaX (effective Washington-type Bernoulli-unit lemma; input of counts II/III and V) — no counterexample
- Statement (AV/LevelCohomology/UnramifiedCocycleIsCoboundaryOfBernoulliUnit.lean:14-27, proved as lemmaX, AV/LevelCohomology/LemmaX.lean:78-304): XData l d φ ξ (φ primitive mod odd f with ord φ | l^d − 1, 4 | l^d − 1, ξ primitive mod 2^n, f·4^(v₂(l^d−1)) ≤ 2^n, φξ odd) ⇒ ‖B_{1,φξ}‖_l = 1 for Q̄_l-valued φ, ξ (genB 1 = Σ_{a≤N} χ(a)(a/N − 1/2) = B_{1,χ}, MapGenBChar.lean:9-12). It feeds CorX (corX, LevelCohomology/ChrInvNeg.lean:197-211: residual unramified cocycles for the characters φξ are coboundaries), used by countII/III (DTC/AdjointLiftingOfCohomology.lean:37) and count V (DTC/LinkedUnlE.lean:61, X.xpp).
- Why a target: Washington's theorem (l-part of class numbers bounded in a cyclotomic Z_p-extension, Invent. Math. 49 (1978)) gives "B_{1,φξ} is an l-unit for all ξ of 2-power conductor 2^n with n ≥ n₀(φ, l)"; LemmaX asserts it with the explicit n₀ = log₂(f·4^v), v = v₂(l^d − 1). A failure would refute a kernel-checked theorem.
- Test (code/lemmax_probe.py): λ | l divides B_{1,χ} for some embedding ⇔ gcd(P, Φ_M) ≠ 1 over GF(l), P(ζ_M) = N·B_{1,χ} (l ∤ N = f·2^n); Galois conjugates of (φ, ξ) satisfy XData too, so one gcd covers all embeddings. - Below the threshold (n < n₀, logs/lemmax_below.log): 16 of 3801 cases divisible (l ≤ 59, f ≤ 37 prime or 1), all with residue degree F = ord_M(l) ≤ 2, e.g. l = 17, f = 1, n = 6; l = 5, f = 13, n = 4; l = 41, f = 5, n = 5.

- Reading: the bound f·4^v ≤ 2^n forces F = ord_M(l) ≥ 2^(n−v) ≥ 2^v·f, where heuristically divisibility has probability ~ (φ(M)/F)·l^(−F); the threshold sits just above the observed failures. ## P3. Small l, residually reducible (task 3a) — no counterexample possible from known examples
- Every instance of SymplecticFourImageDense is residually reducible by construction (rbar = diag(θ̄, Ψ̄, ᾱ, 1), DecompSigmaOrd.lean:69-71); Good only excludes l = 2 (:95). So l = 3, 5, 7 are covered.
- A counterexample needs an irreducible EPt point known NOT to be automorphic, or a positive-dimensional fixed-weight ordinary deformation ring. Neither is known for any geometric (de Rham, ordinary, regular weight (e+1, e, 1, 0)) representation: Fontaine–Mazur–Langlands predicts all such points automorphic and the fixed-weight ordinary ring finite over O. The conclusions are conjecturally TRUE; they are new as theorems, not refutable by a known example.
- NoEnd concerns points r₁ ⊕ r₁^∨θ with r₁ an irreducible lift of θ̄ ⊕ ᾱ; det r₁(c) = θα(c) = α(c)²Ψ(c) = 1 (Psi_even, :104), so r₁ would be an EVEN 2-dimensional geometric representation with distinct weights (e+1, 1): conjecturally nonexistent (Fontaine–Mazur; Calegari 2011 proves it under residual-image hypotheses not met here). Consistent, unproven in this residually reducible setting.

## P4. Uniformity of the polynomial bounds vs Bloch–Kato sizes (task 3c) — no contradiction possible, no known support
- Components are on the EVEN side (PsiEven: ψ(−1) = (−1)^e, so ψ⁻¹χ^(−e) is even, AUT.md §4): Ш_ψ^∨ ≅ Ш²(Z_l(1+e)(ψ)) ⊂ H²(Z[1/lN], Z_l(1+e)(ψ)), whose order (Main Conjecture + Soulé–Deligne elements; Huber–Kings, Burns–Greither) is the index of cyclotomic elements in H¹(Z_l(1+e)(ψ)), i.e. the l-adic valuation of an l-adic L-value OUTSIDE the interpolation range (ψ = 1: ζ_l(1+e), K_{2e}(Z)_l; e = 1: odd parts of K₂(O_F)). These values are not rational numbers, so there is no archimedean height bound; a polynomial bound log #Ш_ψ ≤ (l·N·e)^C is neither predicted in a provable form nor contradicted (Vandiver/Greenberg heuristics predict Ш_ψ = 0 for most (l, e, ψ)).
- The odd-side analogue (rational L-values) is harmless: for χ odd of conductor N, v_l(B_{k,χ}/k) ≤ log|N(B_{k,χ}/k)|/log l ≲ φ(ord χ)·k·log(kN)/log l, polynomial; an L-value divisible by a large power of l (the brief's CM example) can only reach such polynomial sizes, inside (l·N·e)^C for C ≥ 2. CM example (code/cm_lval_probe.py, logs/cm_lval.log, PARI): over the 60,800 imaginary quadratic (CM) fields Q(√−D), D < 2·10⁵, L(0, χ_{−D}) = 2h/w reaches 3⁶ ∣ h(−178559) = 729, 5³ ∣ h(−11519) = 125, 7³ ∣ h(−52631) = 343: large powers of l, yet v_l(h) ≤ 0.78·log_l(√D·log D) throughout, i.e. logarithmic in the conductor, far inside any polynomial bound.
- Known unconditional bounds for the even side (Soulé 1999, Perfect forms and the Vandiver conjecture: torsion of K_n(Z) via homology of SL_N(Z)) are super-polynomial in e (from reduction theory of SL_N(Z), N ≍ e); VWin with e < x^401 cannot use them, so the counts' uniformity is load-bearing and new.
