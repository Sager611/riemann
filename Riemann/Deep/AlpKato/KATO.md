## A. LemmaK

### A1. Statement (AV/GeneratorCountTuples/LemmaK.lean:11-17)
```
def HasMuQ (l : ℕ) (L : Type) [Field L] : Prop := ∃ ζ : L, IsPrimitiveRoot ζ (if l = 2 then 4 else l)
def kBound (C : ℝ) (e n D : ℕ) : ℝ := Real.exp ((2 * (e : ℝ) * n) ^ C * Real.log (2 * (D : ℝ)))
def LemmaK (CK : ℝ) : Prop :=
  ∀ (l : ℕ) [Fact l.Prime] (L Ω : Type) [Field L] [NumberField L] [Field Ω] [Algebra L Ω] [IsAlgClosure L Ω]
    (D : LData L Ω l), IsTotallyComplex L → HasMuQ l L → ∀ e : ℕ, 1 ≤ e →
      Finite (H2 (zlTw D ((e : ℤ) + 1))) ∧
        Real.log (Nat.card (H2 (zlTw D ((e : ℤ) + 1)))) ≤ kBound CK e (Module.finrank ℚ L) (discr L).natAbs
```
- CK is EXISTENTIAL: the route uses `lemmaK_litN_alt (hN) : ∃ CK, LemmaK CK` (DTC/KFinal.lean:11, := lemmaK_of_litN_alt hN bOdd). kBound is increasing in C, so ∃ CK, LemmaK CK = "some fixed exponent C works for all l, L, e".

### A2. The objects (unfolded)
- LData L Ω l (Formalism/GaloisCohomology/Statement.lean:54-64): S : Finset of finite primes of O_L with hS : S = Above L l = {v : l ∈ v} (:50), so S = S_l is FIXED (the primes above l; archimedean places are never in NS, so they may ramify); A v : ValuationSubring Ω over v; chi : GS Ω S →* ℤ_[l]ˣ with hchi : chi ∘ toGS = cycZ L Ω l. Since toGS is surjective, chi is the l-adic cyclotomic character of G_{L,S}.
- cycZ (ReciprocityTheory/GaloisValuations/Global.lean:90-92) = Mathlib cyclotomicCharacter Ω l ∘ (G_L → RingAut Ω); continuous (:98-99).
- GS Ω S = G_L ⧸ NS, NS = topological closure of the normal closure of all inertia groups at places over v ∉ S (Global.lean:124-141): GS = Gal(L_S/L), L_S the maximal extension unramified outside S_l ∪ {∞}.
- zlTw D n = scalRep ℤ_[l] (chi ^ n) (Statement.lean:71-73): Z_l(n), the n-th Tate twist.
- H2 rho = Z2 ⧸ B2 (ReciprocityTheory/GaloisValuations/SeparatedDvrSecondCohomologyMem.lean:68-102): CONTINUOUS 2-cocycles G×G → M modulo d1 of continuous 1-cochains: Tate's continuous cochain cohomology. With M = Z_l (l-adic topology) this is H²_cts(G_{L,S_l}, Z_l(n)) = lim_ν H²(O_L[1/l], μ_{l^ν}^{⊗n}) (the H¹(Z/l^ν) are finite, so no lim¹ term).
- So, in standard notation: LemmaK CK ⟺ for every prime l, every totally complex number field L with μ_l ⊂ L (μ_4 if l = 2) and every e ≥ 1: H²(O_L[1/l], Z_l(e+1)) is finite and
  log #H²(O_L[1/l], Z_l(e+1)) ≤ exp((2en)^CK · log 2D) = (2D)^((2en)^CK),  n = [L:Q], D = |d_L|.
- The parameter 2en = m·n with m = 2e is the K-theory degree: H²(O_L[1/l], Z_l(e+1)) ≅ K_{2e}(O_L) ⊗ Z_l (l odd: Soulé surjectivity 1979 + Rost–Voevodsky; l = 2, L totally complex: Rognes–Weibel 2000).

### A4. Where the strength of STors comes from (HermiteNormalForms/, read at statement + key-step level)
- torsAll (ClassCounting/ExponentLe.lean:70-74, C = 24) := KT.tors_le (HermiteNormalForms/Assembly.lean:25-31: KH.mainFace, a Gabber-type bound for an acyclic G-complex with finitely many orbits of simplices and finite stabilizers, on the complex Sx L N) + KT.orbnum (Orbnum.lean:80: orbBound ≤ (2D·nN)^{12(p+1)(nN)³N}, pure arithmetic, :6-78) + stabilizer bound 3^{nN²}.
- The complex: HS L N = tuples (h_w) of N×N Hermitian matrices over the infinite places (SmulStrictMono.lean:21-32), q h y = Σ_w Re(y_w* h_w y_w) (:59-63), Ry = {h : q h y ≥ 1 ∀ y ∈ O_L^N∖0} (:81), vertices Vx = extreme points of Ry (= perfect forms, SmulNeZeroLat.lean:51), simplices Sx = vertex tuples on a common face (ConeExtensionAcyclic.simp, :76). This is the Voronoi–Koecher–Ash perfect-form complex, as in Soulé 2003.
- The orbit count (ColsMinSmul.lean:34-75, KT.orb): every simplex has N common minimal vectors forming A with det A ≠ 0 (sx_basis :13-21); nrmle gives |N(det A)| ≤ I0 = D^N (nN)^{nN}; hnf (Hnf.lean:7) gives ≤ I0^{1+nN} classes of such A modulo GL_N(O_L); for a fixed A, nearcoord (Nearcoord.lean:10-12) bounds |(B y)_a|_w ≤ RR = N^N I0² at every place w for every minimal vector y of every perfect form having A's columns as minimal vectors (B = adjugate-type, AB = |N det A|·1), via a per-place lower bound det(A_w* h_w A_w) ≥ 1/I0 (from nrmle) and Cramer; points of O_L are 1-separated in max_w|·|_w, so ≤ (2RR+1)^{nN} candidates and ≤ ((2RR+1)^{nN}+1)^{nN²} perfect forms per A (nearvx).
- Difference from Soulé 2003: Soulé puts a GL_N(A)-translate of every minimal-vector set M(h) in ONE compact set of A^N⊗R (his Prop. 2), whose size is exp(c·R(F)) through the unit regulator (his constant C3 = exp(s(4r(log 3r)³)^{s−1}2^{r₂−1}R(F)) in §1), hence his log log k(m) ≤ 2^20 m⁴ log m r^{4r} √D log(D)^{r−1} (Prop. 4 i). The archive counts relative to a basis A of common minimal vectors and counts the A's only up to GL_N(O_L) (HNF classes of bounded |N det|), so no unit-lattice skew enters: polynomial in D. The argument looks sound at this level (my check: for N = 1, μ_w = λ_w|a_w|² ≤ 1 and ∏_w μ_w ≥ c|N a|/√D by Minkowski force every μ_w ≥ c/√D).

### B2. The objects (unfolded)

- Bcr l := (PP l).R.B, PP l := (exists_locPrime_tied l).choose (FiniteCohomology.lean:80-86); LocTied (Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/AdicCompletionLocal/LocTied.lean:11-) ties it by a Galois- and Frobenius-equivariant ring isomorphism eB to the archive's concrete Per.Bcris l; the concrete Per.Bcris was not audited here.

So tw W cyc^(−e) ≅ V_f (Brauer–Nesbitt + Chebotarev; W is continuous by Used.level, Level.cont) and W ≅ V_f(e): pure of weight 0, Hodge–Tate weights ±e. - cycBar ℚ Ω l: the l-adic cyclotomic character with values in Q̄_l^× (several namesakes in the archive; the one resolved in KatoTwoW.lean was not pinned, Formalism.ConjugateSelfDual.cyc (Statement.lean:10-15) has the same formula).
- WitAQ ι r (ModularityArgument/AutomorphyLifting/AAcrossOf.lean:17-20): for every CM quadratic extension K of the base (here: every imaginary quadratic K) on which r stays irreducible, WitA ι (r|G_K), an automorphy witness over K. - Af.tw r χ = r ⊗ χ (BlochKatoSelmerVanishes.lean:22-27); tw W 1 = W.
- Af.Level l M m W (AV/AutomorphicForms/BlochKatoSelmerVanishes.lean:89-115): W continuous (cont, :112); away from l, inertia acts diagonally through finite-order characters killing μ_M; at l, W is upper triangular on the decomposition group with inertia characters cyc^{m_i}·χ_i (χ_i finite order, killing μ_M) and either non-exceptional or potentially crystalline: potentially ordinary.
- Used (KatoTwoOfUseN.lean:11-41): l odd; e ≥ 1; (l−1) ∤ 2e; K imaginary quadratic with l ∤ d_K h_K; N ≥ 2 even, (d_K, N) = 1; θ = the quadratic character of K (ker θ = G_K, θ² = 1); ψ of finite order prime to l, killing μ_N; (−1)^e ψ(c) = 1; W of level N|d_K|, Hodge–Tate type (e, −e), ordinary at l; det W = θ; trace condition on inertia at l; W residually reducible: tr W ≡ θ ω^e ψ + (ω^e ψ)^(−1) mod m (red); a flag on the decomposition group at l; W irreducible on G_{K(μ_{l^∞})} (irr, which excludes forms with CM by K).
- Consequences: det(W ⊗ cyc^(−e)) = θ cyc^(−2e), so f has weight 2e+1 and nebentypus χ_K; tw W θ = W ⊗ det W⁻¹ ≅ W* ≅ V_{f̄}(e). So the conclusion is H¹_f(Q, V_f(e)) = 0 ∧ H¹_f(Q, V_{f̄}(e)) = 0.
