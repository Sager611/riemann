# Borsuk ℝ⁴: dead nodes

One line per dead node: id — smallest reason it cannot work — evidence (round).

- D4a — finite sets on S³_r with r > D/√2 have χ(G) ≤ 4 (Kupavskii arXiv:1306.3910 Thm 1) — R001.
- D6 — OpenAI's obstruction lives on the continuum X₄ = {uuᵀ} ⊂ ℝ⁹; real projector spaces have dimension k(k+1)/2 − 1 ∈ {2, 5, 9}; 4-flat sections of X₃ are conics/line pairs of ℝP² (≤ 2 orthogonal partners each); ℂP¹, ℍP¹ are round spheres — R001.
- D8 — every complete join A ∨ B in an ℝ⁴ diameter graph has χ ≤ 5 (lemma L1: A, B on orthogonal concentric spheres, R_A² + R_B² = D²) — R001.
- D3 — two-distance sets in ℝ⁴ have ≤ 10 points (g(4) = 10, Szöllősi–Östergård arXiv:1804.06040) and every 6-critical ℝ⁴ diameter graph has ≥ 11 vertices (L4: Gallai + L1–L3) — R001, R002.
- P5 — Tolmachev's 4-parameter outer bodies H(u) are near-critical for 5 parts: β₅(H(1,0,0,0)) ≥ 0.9699 (643 points, kissat + DRAT), no convex 5-cell partition below 1.007–1.07 found at hard u; regaining margin needs 4–5-contact normal forms with 6–10 parameters, far beyond the 1.2·10⁴-box 6-part computation — R003, R006.
- P3 — the same method as P5 (contact reduction, parameter boxes, exact enclosures); same barrier — R006.
- P1 — a universal cover is the 0-parameter case of P5's normal forms, so it cannot beat the near-critical H(u); known 4D covers need 16 (cube) or ≥ 6 (Jung ball, volume bound) parts — R006.
- P2 — proved classes need a single-valued or symmetric diametral map; general constant-width bodies have set-valued ones (normal cones up to 37.8° at Reuleaux-simplex vertices) and no reduction to those classes exists; what remains is P5 — R004, R006.
- P4 — Z₂-index, genus and LS bounds on diametral pairs are capped at 5 by the direction map (lemma L5); topological partitions need a single-valued diametral map (smooth case) — R001, R004, R006.
- D4b — r < 0.5477D: the 5 simplex cones suffice; r ∈ [0.5477, 0.7071]D: all points are contacts, a special case of P5, and the full sphere needs 6 parts (volume) so no fixed partition works — R006.
- D5 — known algebraic constructions are two-distance sets (D3), tensor/projector squares (D6) or cube/lattice subsets (D2) — R006.
- D7 — counterexamples need ≥ 11 points (L4) and the 11-vertex Mycielskian M(K₅) is unrealizable; larger 6-critical graphs are not enumerated and each needs a real-algebraic realizability test — R006.
- D9 — multistart optimisation of β₅/diam in ℝ⁴ peaks at 0.913149 (ball 0.912871); a counterexample needs 1 — R004.
