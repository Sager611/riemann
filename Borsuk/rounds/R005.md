# R005 — D1, D2: exhaustive colouring checks of structured finite families (own computation, 2026-11-28)

Method (Borsuk/code/d1.py, d1v2.py, d1v3.py, d1_cegar.py, cegar.py, d2_lattice.py, tabucol.c): exact integer coordinates (ℤ, ℤ[√2], ℤ[φ]); for a vertex-transitive set P and each squared distance s, any S ⊆ P with diam² S = s can be moved to contain x₀, so S ⊆ L_s = {p : d²(x₀, p) ≤ s} and G(S) is an induced subgraph of H_s[L_s] (pairs at squared distance exactly s). H_s[L_s] 5-colourable (max degree < 5, or a tabu colouring checked edge by edge) ⇒ no counterexample at s. Otherwise CEGAR over compatible S ∋ x₀ (all pairs ≤ s): SAT proposes S, tabu colours it, near-colourings of L_s with S frozen give blocking clauses; vertices are pruned unless their compatible neighbours contain a compatible 5-set. Lattices: same with x₀ = 0 (translation).

## D1 (polytope vertex sets, root systems)
- 57 sets, 2325 thresholds: F4/B4/C4 roots, 24-cell ∪ dual (global check); all 15 Wythoff orbits of A4, B4, F4; 8 of the 15 H4 orbits (n = 120 … 3600, including the 600-cell and the 120-cell).
- 2257 thresholds: H_s[L_s] 5-colourable. 68 not: CEGAR UNSAT at 12 (600-cell at 60° and 90°, 120-cell ×3, F4:12, H4:01 ×2, …), 56 unresolved: local sets of 300–3000 points where the CEGAR stalls (H4:01, s = 219.33, |L| = 1236: > 450 iterations, two 300–600 s time-outs).
- Fully resolved (no counterexample among any subsets): the six regular 4-polytopes, all A4, B4, F4 uniform polytopes, the root systems A4, B4, C4, D4, F4, H4.
- Not finished: H4 orbits H4:03, H4:13, H4:012, H4:013, H4:023, H4:123, H4:0123 (2400–14400 points; the single-level check alone ran > 40 min per set).

## D2 (lattice subsets)
- ℤ⁴, s ≤ 24 (24 thresholds); D4, s ≤ 32 (16); A4, s ≤ 12 (6; CEGAR UNSAT at s = 6); A4* scaled by 5, s ≤ 300 (18; CEGAR UNSAT at s = 150): no counterexample.
- A4, s = 14: |L| = 511, 12,360 edges, best near-colouring of L has ≈ 600 conflicts; the CEGAR (166 iterations in 200 s) and its lens variant (> 500 s) did not finish.

## Verdict
- D1, D2 dead: no counterexample wherever the check completed; what remains are 2-level searches (∃ compatible S, ∀ 5-colourings fail) on 300–14,400-point local sets where our solver stalls, and as s grows lattice points become as general as arbitrary finite sets (D7).
