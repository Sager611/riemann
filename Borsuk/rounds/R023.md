# R023 — P12: diameter-1 sets are far easier than outer bodies; fan conjecture (own computation, 2026-11-28)

- Fans: simplex fan = 5 Voronoi cones of a rotated regular simplex (rotation Q, apex c, 10 parameters). Borsuk/code/p12_fan.py: nearly complete diameter-1 sets F (greedy extreme-point completion of a seed, 3,000–4,500 points), then (Q, c) minimising the max piece diameter of F.

| dim | seed | ρ | best fan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | two orthogonal unit triangles (type-3 extreme) | 1/3 | 0.879 |
| 4 | random type-3 contact set | 0.309 | 0.888 |
| 3 | regular tetrahedron (Jung extreme) | 3/8 | 0.764 |
| 3 | unit triangle on a great circle | 1/3 | 0.851 |
| 3 | random 4-contact set | 0.324 | 0.839 |

- The same contact structures give outer bodies with certified β₅ ≥ 0.97–0.98 (R021–R022): the outer-body loss is ≥ 9%. The ball gives √(5/6) = 0.913 for every fan (4D) and 0.888 (3D).
- Blocking measure: two points at angle θ from the apex lie in one cone of a uniformly random fan rotation with probability 7.9% (100°), 5.6% (104.48°), 3.1% (110°), 1.4% (115°), 0.31% (120°), 0.018% (125°), 0 (≥ 130°; the cones' angular diameter is 131.81°). Diametral pairs of a diameter-1 set with circumradius² ρ subtend ≥ arccos(1 − 1/(2ρ)) (120° at ρ = 1/3), so at ρ ≤ 1/3 each diametral chord blocks ≤ 0.31% of rotations.
- Fan conjecture (FC): every diameter-1 set in ℝ⁴ is split by some simplex fan into 5 pieces of diameter < 1. Union-bound heuristics and the data support it; a proof needs control of the 3-parameter family of diametral chords (x(u), x(−u)) of the constant-width completion (x = ∇h is 1-Lipschitz).
