# R008 — P6: density bounds on S³ and the thin-shell lemma near ρ = 3/10 (own analysis, 2026-11-28)

Node: P6. Claims: (1) no density obstruction to 5 parts without unit pairs on the sphere part of Ω; (2) near ρ = 3/10 every diametral pair inside a simplex cone lies in a thin near-contact shell, so the cone partition can only fail through near-contact pairs.

## (1) Densities (todo #8)
- Delsarte LP for measurable A ⊂ S³ avoiding one inner product t: zonal coefficients b_k ≥ 0, Σb_k = 1, Σb_k P_k(t) = 0, P_k(t) = sin((k+1)α)/((k+1) sin α) (n = 4, Gegenbauer λ = 1) gives only the ratio bound m ≤ −P_min/(1 − P_min): 0.40 at α = 131.8° (P₁ = −2/3 is the minimum), 0.30 at α = 115°. Clique bounds: t-cliques have ≤ 3 points for α ≥ 120°, ≤ 2 for α > 120° … so m ≤ 1/3 at best. No bound below 1/5: density does not forbid 5 parts without unit pairs.
- Islands: a colour class on S³ is a union of components of angular diameter < α, pairwise more than α apart; at most 3 components (4 unit vectors have average inner product ≥ −1/3). Volume (Lévy): 5 colours × 3 islands × cap(α/2) ≥ 1 for α ≥ 105°, so volume allows it.
- S₅-equivariant designs (colour class = core around v_c + islands) fail: islands of colour c must lie within (180° − α) − (core radius) of −v_c, forcing cores of radius < 55° that cover only 60% of S³.

## (2) Thin-shell lemma
- Let K have constant width 1, circumradius R (ρ = R²), and split it by 5 simplex cones from the circumcentre (cell angular diameter arccos(−2/3) = 131.8°). If x, y ∈ K lie in one cone and |x − y| = 1, then |x|, |y| ≥ r_min(R) = (−(4/3)R + √((16/9)R² − 4(R² − 1)))/2 and their angle is near 131.8°: from 1 ≤ |x|² + |y|² + (4/3)|x||y|. At ρ = 0.31: r_min = 0.5387 against R = 0.5568 (shell of width 0.018).
- So the cone partition fails only through diametral pairs of near-contact points (within 0.018 of the circumsphere) sitting near one of the 15 critical pairs (Petersen pairs) of a cell. If the near-contact set is small (near finitely many contacts), a generic rotation works; the open case is a large near-contact set.
