## Own: sets containing a regular unit simplex W
- Jung equality: such X has circumcentre = centre of W, so X ⊂ Ω₅ = B(0, √(2/5)) ∩ ⋂ᵢ B(wᵢ, 1).
- The 5 cones aligned with W split Ω₅ into parts of diameter ≤ 0.8741 (60,000 boundary samples: 0.8614; SLSQP local maximum from the worst pair: 0.874032). Numerical, margin 12%.
- Hence every 4D Meissner-type body built on the Reuleaux simplex (it contains W) splits into 5 parts: not a counterexample.
- Contrast: Tolmachev's 3-contact body at the same corner, H(1,0,0,0), has β₅ ≥ 0.9849 (R006): the 3-contact reduction, not the sets, is near-critical there.
