# R022 — P8: relaxation, explicit partitions, Jung stability (own computation, 2026-11-28)

- Relaxed (cluster-consistent) encoding does not help at the extreme type-3 body: 4,000 samples, ε = 0.05: plain UNSAT and relaxed UNSAT (27 far region pairs, 24,667 exempt pairs of 987,658). The obstruction lives in pairs at distance 0.95–1.05, which no relaxation can exempt (Borsuk/code/p8_relsat2.py, streamed CNF).
- H(0.3,0,0,0) (ρ = 0.3178): β₅(12k sample) ∈ [0.95, 0.98] (kissat UNSAT at 0.95; 5-colouring at 0.98).
- Explicit partitions from SAT colourings: power diagrams with k cells per colour class fitted by LP to the 0.99 colouring of the extreme type-3 body (Borsuk/code/p8_multifit.py) give class diameters 1.03–1.10 for k = 1–4 (dense samples). SAT colourings are not geometrically regular; an explicit partition needs regularised colourings.
- Jung stability (type-5 contact simplices, SLSQP): the shortest contact edge can be 0.535 at ρ = 0.38 ("split vertex": unit triangle + two nearby points on its unit circle), 0.93 at ρ = 0.395, ≈ 0.98 at ρ = 0.398. Near-regularity holds only for ρ ≥ 0.395.
- Consequence for P8: a certified proof needs (i) explicit partitions with ≥ 1–2% margin in the H(u) family away from u* and (ii) a 10-parameter type-5 analysis for ρ ∈ [0.375, 0.395]; both are open.
