# R009 — P9: normal cones and rotations near ρ = 3/10 (own analysis, 2026-11-28)

Node: P9. Claim: the 5 simplex cones (a Hadwiger partition with radial normals) are exactly critical at ρ = 3/10; a rotation away from the dangerous diametral pairs extends this to ρ ≤ 3/10 + ε₀ for some ε₀ > 0; beyond that cones are hopeless. Verdict: P9 gives at most a small analytic extension; the middle range needs partitions adapted to the diametral structure.

## Normal-cone bound (constant width 1, circumradius R, centre 0)
- For x ∈ ∂K with |x| = s and a normal n ∈ N(x), the partner x − n lies in K ⊂ B(0, R): ⟨x, n⟩ ≥ (s² + 1 − R²)/(2s), so angle(n, x/|x|) ≤ arccos((s² + 1 − R²)/(2s)), largest at s = R: arccos(1/(2R)) (24.1° at ρ = 0.3, 26.1° at 0.31, 37.8° at 0.4).
- Equality needs the partner on the circumsphere: the extreme tilts come from contact–contact diametral pairs (both points at radius R, angle 2 arcsin(1/(2R))).
- Radial assignment (cones) separates every diametral pair iff 2 arccos(1/(2R)) ≤ 180° − 131.8°, i.e. ρ ≤ 3/10 (equality case of R002's bound).
- Canonical normals (centre of the smallest cap containing N(x)) separate a diametral pair (x, y) if θ_N(x) + θ_N(y) < 48.2°, θ_N = angular radius of the normal cone; partners of a point with a large cone lie on a spherical face of S(x, 1) and have single normals, so only pairs of two singular points are critical.

## Rotation avoidance
- Diametral pairs of K form a 3-parameter family {(x, x − n)}; putting one pair (directions at angle ψ < 131.8°) into one simplex cone is a codimension-5 condition up to a tolerance η(ψ) → 0 as ψ → 131.8°. The bad rotations have measure O(η²) × (size of the near-contact diametral family), so a good rotation exists for ρ ∈ [3/10, 3/10 + ε₀] with some explicit ε₀ > 0 (constants not yet computed).
- For ρ ≥ 0.32 the angles of near-contact diametral pairs reach 2 arcsin(1/(2R)) ≤ 124°, far inside the 131.8° cells: random rotations fail with high probability (numerics R007: 1.00–1.01 at ρ ≈ 0.31).
