# R028 — merging lemma; 6-piece merging fails (own computation, 2026-11-28)

- Merging lemma (own, elementary): let P₁, …, P_k be a closed cover of Ω with diam Pᵢ < 1, X ⊂ Ω compact with diam X = 1, and G_X the graph on [k] with i ~ j iff X has a, b with a ∈ Pᵢ, b ∈ Pⱼ, |a − b| = 1. If χ(G_X) ≤ 5, the unions of same-coloured pieces split X into 5 parts of diameter < 1 (pairs inside one piece are < 1; pairs across same-coloured pieces are ≠ 1 and ≤ 1). With k = 6, X fails only if G_X = K₆.
- Test (Borsuk/code/p13_merge.py): 6-cell power diagrams optimised on samples (max piece 0.996 for the extreme type-3 body, 0.971 for H(0.8,0,0,0)); a greedy adversary realises K₆ by unit pairs with 23 and 27 points, keeping most of the pool. Six convex pieces are far too coarse; with fine pieces the lemma becomes the cell-level 2-level problem (D10's CEGAR).
