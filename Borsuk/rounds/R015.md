# R015 — P8: first certifier prototype (own code, 2026-11-28)

- Borsuk/code/cert.py: polytope enclosure of (balls ∩ halfspaces ∩ cell) by tangent halfspaces with rational normals and rational upper bounds of the radii terms (integer square root, outward), refined by cutting planes at vertices outside a ball (float vertex enumeration by Qhull); final bound in exact rationals: outward perturbation of the right-hand sides (polytope only grows, becomes simple), vertices by pivoting from one vertex (graph connected, Balinski), diam² = max over vertex pairs; asserts on degeneracy and unboundedness.
- Test: ball ρ = 0.29 ∩ one simplex cone (true diam² = (10/3)ρ = 0.9667): 483 halfspaces, 2,843 vertices, bound 1.0199, 279 s. Correct but too loose (12 rounds × 40 planes) and too slow for ≥ 10⁵ boxes.
- Next: (a) refine until every vertex is within 10⁻³ of its ball (adaptive plane placement); (b) port the pivot traversal to C with exact 128-bit or GMP-free integer arithmetic on scaled rationals; (c) avoid polytope enclosure of sphere patches altogether: bound |x − y|² ≤ |x|² + |y|² − 2⟨x, y⟩ with ⟨x, y⟩ bounded below on pairs of small spherical patches (interval arithmetic), i.e. a pairwise branch-and-bound on a mesh of the cell's boundary.
