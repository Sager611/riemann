# R024 — P12: most fan rotations already work (own computation, 2026-11-28)

- Random rotations, apex = circumcentre (Borsuk/code/p12_randrot.py, 300–400 Haar rotations): fraction of rotations whose 5 pieces all have diameter < 0.999:

| set | ρ | n | < 0.999 | < 0.99 | best | median |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| completion of a type-4 contact set | – | 4,504 | 100% | 100% | 0.894 | 0.940 |
| completions of type-5 contact sets (2) | 0.31 | 4,505 | 100% | 99.8–100% | 0.887–0.890 | 0.949 |
| completion of two orthogonal unit triangles | 1/3 | 4,506 | 96% | 85.5% | 0.901 | 0.967 |
| 4D body of revolution of the Reuleaux triangle (tip + singular 2-sphere of contacts; needs exactly 5 colours) | 1/3 | 5,281 | 90.7% | 84.3% | 0.863 | 0.956 |

- Optimised fans with free apex on 4,500-point completions: 0.892–0.930 (Borsuk/code/p12_fan.py).
- The adversary "add a pool point or pool pair at distance ≥ 0.998 inside a cone of the current best fan" (Borsuk/code/p12_adv.py) finds no candidates: the best fan is robust for every completion inside the pool.
- Spherical analogue (Borsuk/code/p12_sph.py): D ⊂ S³ with angular diameter ≤ θ and 0 ∈ conv D, greedy completions; good rotations 89% (θ = 120°), 74.5% (115°), 99% (125°); best fans keep every piece within 77–84°.
- Cap example: a 60° cap of the circumsphere at ρ = 1/3 blocks 19% of rotations about 0, but its own circumcentre is elsewhere (circumradius 1/2), so it is not a valid configuration.
- Fan failures need diametral chords subtending < 131.8° at the circumcentre; both endpoints then lie in the shell |x| ≥ s_min(ρ) (0.539 at ρ = 0.31, 0.518 at 1/3, 0.494 at 0.36, 0.46 at 0.4). At ρ slightly above 0.3 the condition is a Petersen-graph occupancy problem for the 10 critical directions w_ab.
