## Own lemmas (exact, used for deaths)
L1 (joins). If every a ∈ A is at distance D = diam X from every b ∈ B (A, B ⊂ X ⊂ ℝ⁴), then A lies on a sphere of radius R_A in its affine hull F_A, B lies on the sphere of radius √(D² − R_A²) centred at the circumcentre of A in the orthogonal complement of F_A, and symmetrically. A planar point set on a circle of radius R with pairwise distances ≤ D has an odd cycle of diameters only if R ≤ D/√3 (else all points lie on an arc of angle < 120° and at most one pair is at distance D). Case split on (dim F_A, dim F_B) ⇒ χ(G[A ∪ B]) ≤ 5 for every complete join.
L2 (edge links). N(a) ∩ N(b) lies on a 2-sphere of radius (√3/2)D in the bisector hyperplane: angular diameter ≤ 2 arcsin(1/√3) < 90°, so diameter arcs pairwise cross (spherical Hopf–Pannwitz: quadrilateral and in-triangle cases, distance from a point is convex within π/2); gnomonic projection makes G[N(a) ∩ N(b)] a straight-line thrackle: ≤ one cycle, odd ⇒ χ ≤ 3. Triangle links lie on a circle of radius √(2/3)D: ≤ 1 edge.
L3 (vertex links). N(x) ⊂ S³(x, D) with diameter ≤ D, D > D/√2 ⇒ χ(G[N(x)]) ≤ 4 (Kupavskii Thm 1).
L4 (≥ 11 points). A 6-critical diameter graph in ℝ⁴ on ≤ 10 vertices is a join G₁ ∨ G₂ (Gallai) with (χ₁, χ₂) ∈ {(1,5), (2,4), (3,3)}: excluded by L3, L2, L1. So every counterexample has ≥ 11 points.
L5 (topology). For a complete bipartite A × B in a diameter graph, 0 ∉ conv{a − b}: else the weighted centroids of A and B coincide, so Var_p(A) + Var_q(B) = D², while all pairs within A and within B are ≤ D gives Var_p(A) + Var_q(B) ≤ D²(1 − (Σp² + Σq²)/2) < D². Hence (u, v) ↦ (u − v)/|u − v| extends to a Z₂-map B(G) → S³ and ind B(G) ≤ 3: topological lower bounds never exceed 5 in ℝ⁴.
