# R003 — P5: can the outer bodies H(u) be cut into 5 parts of diameter < 1? (own computation)

Node: P5. Claim tested: some H(u) needs 6 parts (would kill P5). Verdict so far: no obstruction found; evidence points to 5-partitionable H(u).

## Exact obstructions ruled out
- Cliques: by Rankin, any 6 vectors in ℝ⁴ have a pair with ⟨x, y⟩ ≥ 0, so 6 points of B(0, √r) have a pair at squared distance ≤ 2r ≤ 4/5 < 1. No 6 points of H(u) are pairwise ≥ 1 apart.
- Topology: for complete bipartite A × B in the far graph (pairs ≥ 1) of H(u), Var_p(A) + Var_q(B) ≤ 2r < 1 ≤ E|a − b|², so 0 ∉ conv{a − b}: the box complex maps to S³ and topological bounds give at most 5.
- Volume: a set of angular diameter θ on S³ has at most the volume of a cap of radius θ/2; 5 such caps cover S³ only if θ ≥ 121°. Hence the full ball B(0, √r) needs 6 parts once r > 0.33 (2 arcsin(1/(2√r)) < 121°); the far graph on 800 random points of the sphere |x|² = 0.35 is not 5-colourable (SAT). H(u) avoids this: it keeps only part of the sphere.

## Numerics (Borsuk/.scratch/p5_cegar.py, tabucol.c)
- CEGAR on boundary samples of H(u): colour a seed set Y (tabu search, SAT fallback), add candidates with a far neighbour in every colour class, then extend greedily to all candidates.
- u = (0.6, 1, 0, 0) (ρ = 0.3349, unit equilateral contact triangle at height 0.039, the configuration where 6 cones and 5 balls both fail): joint 5-colouring of the far graph on 40,000 boundary points at threshold 1 (seed set 852 points).
- Random-sample lower bounds β₅(sample) ≈ 0.85–0.91 (700–900 points) at several u.
