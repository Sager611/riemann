# R025 — P12: adversaries cannot block fans; pairwise compatibility is too weak (own computation, 2026-11-28)

- Set-cover adversary (Borsuk/code/p12_adv2.py): grow X (diameter ≤ 1, inside B(0,R), from a contact seed) by unit pairs drawn from a shell pool, each pair chosen to lie in one cone of as many of 300 random fan rotations (apex 0) as possible. ρ = 1/3 (unit great-circle triangle seed): stuck after blocking 30 and 36 of 300 rotations (pool of admissible unit pairs exhausted; 21–25 points); free fan optimisation on the final X gives 0.635–0.670. Random type-4/5 seeds (ρ ≈ 0.31): no admissible blocking pair at all.
- Spherical analogue (Borsuk/code/p12_sph_adv.py, pairs at angle ∈ [θ − 0.3°, θ]): blocked 89/300 (θ = 115°), 63/300 (120°), 28/300 (125°); best fans for the final sets 64°, 81°, 82°.
- Critical zone of one fan at ρ = 1/3 (shell points with a same-cone partner at distance ≥ 1 inside B(0,R)): 1.8% of the shell, 0.63% of the ball.
- FC-k by pairwise compatibility (Borsuk/code/p12_fck.py): with 12 random rotations at ρ = 1/3, 93% of (rotation, cone) type pairs are compatible, so a pairwise relaxation cannot certify anything; the obstruction to blocking is k-wise (all chosen points pairwise within 1).
