# R014 — P8: a fourth contact (own computation, 2026-11-28)

- H(u) ∩ B(P₄, 1), random admissible P₄ below the triangle (|P₄| = √ρ, |P₄ − Pᵢ| ≤ 1, x₃ < 0), 8,000 boundary samples, strict tabu CEGAR (Borsuk/code/p8_four.py): at u = (0.6, 1, 0, 0) and (0.7, 0, 1, 0), all 11 configurations have β₅(sample) ∈ (0.94, 0.97] — the same room as the all-contact survey (R007).
- So one more contact buys little; room stays 3–6% on 8k samples (less on denser samples). A certified 5-part computation must work at ~1–3% room over at least 6 parameters (4 for the triangle form + 2–3 for P₄).
- Box-count estimate: room m ≈ 0.02 needs parameter boxes with enclosure growth ≲ m/2, i.e. widths ≈ 0.01–0.03 per coordinate: ≈ 10⁵–10⁷ boxes in 4 parameters, 10⁸–10¹¹ in 6–7. Only an adaptive scheme (extra parameters only where the 4-parameter form fails) can be affordable, and only with a per-box cost of milliseconds (C certifier, warm-started partitions).
