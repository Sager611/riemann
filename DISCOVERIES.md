Strict: genuinely new theorems, records, or counterexamples to established statements only.

- New unconditional zero-density estimate for ring class L-functions: for a negative discriminant D, Σ over the ring class characters χ of D of N(σ; t) (zeros β+iγ with β ≥ σ, |γ − t| ≤ 1) ≪_ε |D|^{2σ(1−σ)+ε}(|t|+3)^3. Every published density bound for this family is weaker in |D| for each σ in (1/2, 1) (Pascadi–Thorner 2025, Kowalski–Michel 2002). Method classical (Carlson detector + weighted large sieve over primitive ideals); the statement is new. Paper proof, not refereed, not Lean. [note](Riemann/Deep/Combos/NOTE.md) Reviewer: core argument correct (weighted large sieve; per unit window). Online: arXiv sweep of ~105 queries found nothing implying it ([novelty](Riemann/Deep/Combos/NOVELTY.md)); adversarial re-check (Zenodo, Hexagon series, OpenAI, ATLAS, GitHub, classical papers; exponents compared at σ = 3/4 and 0.9) still new ([novelty 2](Riemann/Deep/Combos/NOVELTY2.md)).
- Small record: certified Weil positivity on half-width 3/2 — Q(f) ≥ 10^{−100}‖f‖² for every complex C² test function supported in [−3/2, 3/2] (Arb ball arithmetic; chain in PROOF.md). Beyond the previous certified window, half-width 1.495 (Elston, Zenodo 23174403, which we audited as rigorous); gain +0.005. Not refereed, not Lean, not progress toward RH. [report](Riemann/Deep/WeilRecord/REPORT2.md) Reviewer: rigorous at proof-chain level, conditional on the reported interval bounds. Online: largest posted certificate found was Elston 1.495.
- Least primes represented by forms, for almost all discriminants (unconditional): for every A > 3/2, all but O(X^{1−c}) fundamental discriminants D with |D| ≤ X, negative or positive, have prime ideals in every (narrow, if D > 0) ideal class in every interval (x, x + x|D|^{−κ}] with x ≥ |D|^A, also in prescribed sectors for D < 0; so every primitive binary quadratic form of discriminant D, definite or indefinite, represents a prime ≤ 2|D|^A (κ = 8·10^{−5}, c = 1.7·10^{−4} for A ≥ 1.55; below that they depend on A). Previous: |D|^{20/3+ε} with a density-zero exceptional set, definite forms only (Ditchen 2013); for indefinite forms only a single class was known on average (ny² − x² represents a prime < n for almost all n, via the prime-plus-a-square theorem), not every class. Inputs: our ring class density theorem (above), its real-quadratic analogue, a long-mollifier per-D density (zeros with β < 3/2 − 1/A), the Humphries–Thorner zero detector over D (narrow class characters give tempered Maass forms), genus characters and Siegel zeros handled separately; no 2026 half-plane. The a² + nb² least-prime clause is known in stronger form (Baier–Zhao 2007). Paper proofs, not refereed, not Lean. [note](Riemann/Deep/RCApps2/NOTE.md) [first version](Riemann/Deep/RCApps/NOTE.md) Reviewer: correct (both versions); significant. Online: nothing found states or implies it ([novelty](Riemann/Deep/RCApps/NOVELTY.md)); indefinite case and the 3/2 exponent: adversarial re-check found nothing that states or implies them ([novelty 2](Riemann/Deep/RCApps2/NOVELTY.md)).
- Primes with prescribed Frobenius-trace sign in every ideal class, for almost all discriminants (unconditional): fix E/Q without CM (or any non-CM newform). For every A > 3, all but O(X^{1−c}) fundamental discriminants D with |D| ≤ X, negative or positive, have in every ideal class (narrow if D > 0) primes p with a_E(p) > 0 and primes with a_E(p) < 0 in every interval (x, x + x|D|^{−κ}] with x ≥ |D|^A; so every primitive form of discriminant D represents primes p, p′ ≤ 2|D|^A with a_E(p) > 0 > a_E(p′). One-sided conditions a_E(p) > −α√p need only A > 9/4. Inputs: new zero density for Sym^m f ⊗ θ_χ over class group characters (Theorem C) and over |D| ≍ X (Proposition F); m ≤ 2 only (Gelbart–Jacquet). No bound with any power of |D| was known, per D or on average. Paper proof, not refereed, not Lean.
