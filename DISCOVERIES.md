- New unconditional zero-density estimate for ring class L-functions: for a negative discriminant D, Σ over the ring class characters χ of D of N(σ; t) (zeros β+iγ with β ≥ σ, |γ − t| ≤ 1) ≪_ε |D|^{2σ(1−σ)+ε}(|t|+3)^3. Every published density bound for this family is weaker in |D| for each σ in (1/2, 1) (Pascadi–Thorner 2025, Kowalski–Michel 2002). Method classical (Carlson detector + weighted large sieve over primitive ideals); the statement is new. Paper proof, not refereed, not Lean. [note](Riemann/Deep/Combos/NOTE.md) Reviewer: core argument correct (weighted large sieve; per unit window). Online: arXiv sweep of ~105 queries found nothing implying it ([novelty](Riemann/Deep/Combos/NOVELTY.md)); adversarial re-check (Zenodo, Hexagon series, OpenAI, ATLAS, GitHub, classical papers; exponents compared at σ = 3/4 and 0.9) still new ([novelty 2](Riemann/Deep/Combos/NOVELTY2.md)).
