- New unconditional zero-density estimate for ring class L-functions: for a negative discriminant D, Σ over the ring class characters χ of D of N(σ; t) (zeros β+iγ with β ≥ σ, |γ − t| ≤ 1) ≪_ε |D|^{2σ(1−σ)+ε}(|t|+3)^3. Every published density bound for this family is weaker in |D| for each σ in (1/2, 1) (Pascadi–Thorner 2025, Kowalski–Michel 2002). Method classical (Carlson detector + weighted large sieve over primitive ideals); the statement is new. Paper proof, not refereed, not Lean. - Least primes represented by forms, for almost all discriminants (unconditional): for every A > (3+√69)/10 ≈ 1.1307 (GRH would give 1; first proved for A > 1.6536, then 3/2, then 1.1307 for D < 0, then for D > 0 and sectors too), all but O(X^{1−c}) fundamental discriminants D with |D| ≤ X, negative or positive, have prime ideals in every (narrow, if D > 0) ideal class in every interval (x, x + x|D|^{−κ}] with x ≥ |D|^A, also in prescribed sectors for D < 0; so every primitive binary quadratic form of discriminant D, definite or indefinite, represents a prime ≤ 2|D|^A (c, κ > 0 depend on A; intervals of length H ≤ x). Previous: |D|^{20/3+ε} with a density-zero exceptional set, definite forms only (Ditchen 2013); for indefinite forms only a single class was known on average (ny² − x² represents a prime < n for almost all n, via the prime-plus-a-square theorem), not every class. Inputs: our ring class density theorem (above), its real-quadratic analogue, a long-mollifier per-D density (zeros with β < 3/2 − 1/A), the Humphries–Thorner zero detector over D (narrow class characters give tempered Maass forms), genus characters and Siegel zeros handled separately; no 2026 half-plane. The a² + nb² least-prime clause is known in stronger form (Baier–Zhao 2007). The 1.1307 step uses a new large sieve over pairs (D, χ ∈ Cl(D)^) at family size X^{3/2} (all-orders positivity, a dual lattice-point identity with Hecke relations, Poisson/Mellin, Heath-Brown quadratic large sieve) and a family density X^{(13−8σ)/(7−2σ)+ε} [RCApps3](Riemann/Deep/RCApps3/NOTE.md), reviewed correct in substance (corrections applied), novelty checked ([novelty 3](Riemann/Deep/RCApps3/NOVELTY.md)). The extension to D > 0 and sectors at 1.1307 [RCApps4](Riemann/Deep/RCApps4/NOTE.md) was reviewed correct in substance (units unfolded via nonnegative Fourier weights, finite truncated counts, regulator via h⁺R⁺; Theorem 3⁺ must keep H ≤ x). Paper proofs, not refereed, not Lean. [note](Riemann/Deep/RCApps2/NOTE.md) [first version](Riemann/Deep/RCApps/NOTE.md) Reviewer: correct (both versions); significant. Online: nothing found states or implies it ([novelty](Riemann/Deep/RCApps/NOVELTY.md)); indefinite case and the 3/2 exponent: adversarial re-check found nothing that states or implies them ([novelty 2](Riemann/Deep/RCApps2/NOVELTY.md)).

For every A > 3, all but O(X^{1−c}) fundamental discriminants D with |D| ≤ X, negative or positive, have in every ideal class (narrow if D > 0) primes p with a_E(p) > 0 and primes with a_E(p) < 0 in every interval (x, x + x|D|^{−κ}] with x ≥ |D|^A; so every primitive form of discriminant D represents primes p, p′ ≤ 2|D|^A with a_E(p) > 0 > a_E(p′). - Thin-family large sieve and least primes for CM fields (unconditional): for a fixed totally real field F ≠ Q of degree n and CM extensions K/F, a content-weighted large sieve over the class group characters of K with constant ≈ N + √d_K (via a relative lattice lemma; the regulator factor of F is unnecessary), zero density Σ_χ N(σ; t, χ) ≪ (d_K Tⁿ)^{2(1−σ)/(3−2σ)+ε} per unit window, and: for every A > (3+√69)/10 ≈ 1.1307 (first 3/2, then lowered with a family large sieve over pairs (K, χ ∈ Ĉl_K) at family size X^{3/2}, [CM2](Riemann/Deep/CM2/NOTE.md), reviewed correct in substance, two minor text repairs noted: imprimitive Euler factors cost Q^{O(ε)}, and a conductor bookkeeping point in Lemma 14), almost all CM fields K/F with prime (or rough) relative discriminant have degree-one prime ideals in every ideal class in every interval (x, x + x d_K^{−κ}] with x ≥ d_K^A. No almost-all result was known for F ≠ Q; every-field bounds are d_K^{694} (Thorner–Zaman 2017), GRH d_K^{1+ε}. Lemma 1 is classical and the method is routine (same mechanism as our ring class theorems); the statements are new. Paper proof, not refereed, not Lean.
