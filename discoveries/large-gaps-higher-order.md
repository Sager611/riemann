# Higher-order per-gap inequalities pass the Wirtinger ceiling for large gaps: Λ > 3.367

**Claim.** Λ := lim sup (γ_{n+1} − γ_n)/(2π/log γ_n) > 3.367 = 3367/1000 (order 6, degree-80 amplifier; 3.34 = 167/50 with order 4, degree 64), unconditionally, over consecutive ordinates of zeros of ζ on the critical line (was 3.324, ours). The tool: in Bui–Milinovich's large-gap method, Wirtinger's inequality ∫|f|² ≤ (g/π)²∫|f′|² on each gap is replaced by a per-gap inequality of order 4,
(V) Σ_{j≤4} a_j‖f^{(j)}‖² + φ12[f′f″]_0^g + φ32[f‴f″]_0^g ≥ 0 for every g ∈ (0, π] and every real f ∈ H⁴(0, g) with f(0) = f(g) = 0,
with exact rationals a_0, …, a_4, φ12, φ32 (φ32 = −a_3) whose boundary terms telescope across consecutive gaps. It passes the ceiling of the Wirtinger step (3.32658 in floats, certified failure at 3.335), and per-gap inequalities with derivatives of order ≤ 2 provably add nothing.

**Before.** Λ > 3.324 (ours, gapbar, 12 Nov 2026; audited line by line by gapaudit, 19 Nov 2026), Bui–Milinovich's criterion with the amplifier at the midpoint; 3.18 (Bui–Milinovich 2018). The ceiling 3.32658 (float) and the certified failure at 3.335 (gapceil, gapbar) fix only the first-derivative Wirtinger step. The survey in Bui–Hall–Subirà Jorge (arXiv 2511.14415) lists only inequalities in f and f′ (L² Wirtinger, Beesack's L^{2k} form, Hall's L⁴ form).

**Method.** F(t) = e^{ivtL}ζ(½+it)ζ(½+it+iκπ/L)M(½+it+iκπ/(2L)), L = log(T/2π), M(s) = Σ_{h≤y} d₂(h)P[h]h^{−s}, y = T^ϑ, r = 2, ϑ = 2499/10000, P of degree 64, κ = 167/50, v = 21294/15625. If Λ ≤ κ, every gap of F's real zeros in [T, 2T] is ≤ (1 + o(1))κπ/L (F vanishes at γ and at γ − κπ/L). (V), rescaled by λ = (1 + ε)κ/L and applied to Re F and Im F on each gap, telescoped and summed, gives Σ_j a_jκ^{2j}c_j ≥ 0, where ∫_T^{2T}|F^{(j)}|² = c_jKTL^{(r+2)²+2j}(1 + o(1)) and c_j is Bui–Milinovich's main-term integral with the multiplier (v − S)^{2j}: their Lemma main, uniform for complex shifts ≪ 1/L, differentiated by their own Cauchy-formula argument (printed for j ≤ 2). The certificate shows H := −Σ_j a_jκ^{2j}Q[(v − S)^{2j}] > 0, a contradiction.
