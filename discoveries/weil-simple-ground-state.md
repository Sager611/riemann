# The Weil form of Connes–Consani–Moscovici has a simple even ground state for every λ in [√2, √20] (nodes N13, N1): an independent confirmation (pre-empted by Botha, Zenodo 23852617; moved to COROLLARIES)

**Claim.** Let QW_λ be the Weil quadratic form on L²([λ^{−1}, λ], d*u) of Connes–Consani–Moscovici (arXiv 2511.22755, eq. (3.10)) and A_λ its self-adjoint operator. For every λ ∈ [√2, √20] (c = λ² ∈ [2, 20], half-width 0.347 to 1.498 in log coordinates), the smallest eigenvalue of A_λ is simple and its eigenvector is even (invariant under u → 1/u). At 52 explicit windows (c = 2, 3, …, 20 and the 38 points of a chain) every truncation QW_λ^N with N ≥ N₀ (30 to 160) is even-simple too, so CCM's Theorem 5.10 applies there: the operators D_{λ,N} are self-adjoint and the zeros of the entire function ξ̂ are real and equal their spectrum. This includes CCM's own numerical cases λ = 3, √12, √13, √14 with N = 120.

**Also.** New certified terms of OEIS A401172 (a(n) = ⌊−log₁₀ κ(n)⌋, κ(n) the bottom of Weil's form on (−log(n)/2, log(n)/2); the OEIS lists n ≤ 9): a(10) = 42, a(11) = 48, a(12) = 53, a(13) = 58, a(15) = 69, a(16) = 74, a(18) = 85, a(19) = 90, from certified two-sided brackets (n = 2..9 agree with the OEIS data). n = 14, 17, 20 stay open: κ(n) lies within a few percent of a power of 10 there. The orchestrator reran the n = 10 decision on a copy.

Also the single window λ = e^{0.8} (Zhu, arXiv 2608.24827, same three-inequality scheme) and, non-explicitly, sufficiently small windows (Suzuki, arXiv 2606.09096 Thm 4). Zenodo items certify finite matrices only (e.g. (c, N) = (13, 120)).

**Method.** Three certified inequalities per window: (a) an upper bound U ≥ λ₁^even from the Rayleigh quotient of an explicit even vector g (exact dyadic coefficients; matrix entries in Arb from closed forms with digamma and Hurwitz-zeta values); (b) a lower bound Q ≥ L_odd on all odd functions (Chekmachev's periodized lattice minorant with a Woodbury bound and a Cholesky positivity proof); (c) the same minorant with a rank-one deflation by g, giving Q ≥ L₂ on the even complement of g. If U < min(L_odd, L₂), the spectral subspace below min(L_odd, L₂) is one-dimensional, so the ground state is simple, and (b) makes it even. The bounds hold on all of L², hence on every truncation containing g. Every eigenvalue of each parity sector is non-increasing in c (extension by zero keeps the form), so U(c_i) < L(c_{i+1}) covers all of [c_i, c_{i+1}]; a chain of 37 such steps covers [2, 20]. On the integer windows U is 7.7 (c = 2) to 150 (c = 20) times below the lower bounds; the ground eigenvalue is bracketed within a factor 2 to 5 (about e^{−12.3c}).

**Certificate.** python-flint 0.9.0 Arb plus the proved-rounding node scan of WeilZeta; c = 2 reruns in 6 s (rerun.sh); the whole chain took 26 min, 0.7 GB at c = 20. The orchestrator reran on a copy (6 Nov 2026): the c = 2 window, the chain 2 → 5 and the chain 16 → 20, all ok with the logged values; table.py rechecks all 37 chain steps from logs/chain.jsonl. Matrix entries agree with 50-digit mpmath quadrature to 3.5·10^{−49}; negative controls fail where they must. A bug of the older WeilZeta program was found and avoided: its default 201 kept nodes leaves the odd minorant indefinite at c = 7 and 9 (a failed check proves nothing; 401 nodes fix it).

**Caveat.** Bounded range only: nothing here bears on λ → ∞, where positivity of the bottom for every λ is equivalent to RH, nor on CCM's second missing step (convergence of the approximants). Python and Arb, not formalized. Larger windows are memory-bound (about c = 25 in 1.5 GB).

**RH relevance:** N13 (spectral realization: CCM's self-adjoint approximants D_{λ,N}, whose spectra are the zeros of ξ̂, now exist unconditionally for these windows) and N1 (Weil positivity: the ground state of the Weil form is certified simple and even, with a two-sided bracket).
