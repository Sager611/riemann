# Imaginary quadratic fields with class number at most 10,000 (node N11)

**Claim.** Every imaginary quadratic field K = Q(√−d) (d = |disc K|) with class number h(K) ≤ 10,000 is known, without RH or GRH: there are 410,810,155 of them, and the largest d is 28,154,696,707 (h = 9819). Every h ≤ 10,000 occurs. Counts, largest d and SHA-256 of the sorted lists for N = 1000, 2000, 2500, …, 10,000 are in [REPORT](../Riemann/Deep/ImqClass2/REPORT.md); the per-h table (h, number of fields, largest d) is perh_h10000.txt. Supersedes [class-number-5000](class-number-5000.md) (h ≤ 5000, earlier the same day).

**Before.** h ≤ 5000 unconditionally (ours, ImqClass, 6 Nov 2026); h ≤ 3000 conditional on Kimura's real-zero bound, Lean-checked by its author but unrefereed (M. Jaswal, Zenodo 23547020, 21 Oct 2026); h ≤ 2000 unconditionally (arXiv 2610.21511, Oct 2026, with a Lean-checked zero gap) and under GRH (Garcia–Grenié–Lee–Molteni, arXiv 2610.14257); counts for odd h < 10⁶ under GRH (Holmin–Jones–Kurlberg–McLeman–Petersen, Exp. Math. 2019); h ≤ 100 (Watkins 2004).

**Method.** Three ranges of d, as for h ≤ 5000.
- d ≥ e^27.36 ≈ 7.627·10^11: h(−d) > 10,000 from our explicit Littlewood bound ([littlewood-half-plane](littlewood-half-plane.md)), which rests on the Lean-checked zero-free half-plane Re s > 0.87338196661320752 for every Dirichlet L-function: claim (A), h(−d) ≥ 0.11√d/(π log log d), beyond log d = 27.53, and 17 certified cells of log d in [27.36, 27.53] with Littlewood78's master inequality (threshold.py, loading Littlewood78/cert.py unchanged).
- 2.8·10^10 ≤ d ≤ 7.627·10^11: no field has h ≤ 10,000 except d = 28,154,696,707 (h = 9819, also in the tables). For fundamental −d, h(−d) ≥ M(d), the number of reduced forms from primitive ideals of small norm; a bit-parallel sieve (isieve2.c) certified M(d) ≥ 10,001 for all 275,512,500,000 candidates (every d ≡ 3, 7, 11, 15, 4, 8 mod 16) except 33 tier-4 entries, which Kimura's unconditional classno_upto (t4.gp) decided exactly; 3.5 h on one core.
- d < 111·2^28 = 29,796,335,616: the Mosunov–Jacobson tables (Math. Comp. 85 (2016), unconditional, verified with the Eichler–Selberg trace formula; LMFDB files k = 0..110, 9,056,999,962 entries), extracted with checks (each file's d are exactly the fundamental discriminants of its class and slab; invariant factors multiply to h and form a divisibility chain; gzip CRCs).

**Checks.** Rows N ≤ 5000 identical (count, largest d, SHA-256) to ImqClass, hence N = 1000, 2000 to 2610.21511's lists; counts for all 5000 odd h ≤ 9999 equal Holmin et al.'s GRH counts (36,132,505 fields); PARI for every row's largest d and for 200 random h; in the overlap [2.8·10^10, 2.98·10^10) of sieve and tables the sieve found exactly the one table field with h ≤ 10,000. The orchestrator reran (6 Nov 2026, on copies in /tmp): two sieve slices ([2.8·10^10, 3.0·10^10] and [7.607·10^11, 7.627·10^11]: bad 0; the first slice's 28 tier-4 survivors through t4.gp give exactly d = 28,154,696,707, h = 9819), threshold.py (identical), PARI quadclassunit on all 18 rows' largest d (agree), rows N = 2500..5000 against ImqClass (identical), per-h counts and largest d for h ≤ 100 against OEIS A046125 and A038552 (all equal), per-h total 410,810,155.

**Review.** imqclass2's own reviewer read isieve2.c against the bound (bound, weights and bit-sliced exactness: correct). The orchestrator's reviewer (verdict only, argument inline) first reported a GAP in step 1 (the headline 0.0350√d/max(3, log log d) gives only about 9,237 at the sieve ceiling); that came from my shortened paraphrase: the threshold uses claim (A) only beyond log d = 27.53 and the certified cells below it. Re-review: CLOSED (F is nonincreasing in log d, so each cell is certified conservatively). Verdict: SIGNIFICANT (the unconditional classification doubled from h ≤ 5000 to h ≤ 10,000).

**Novelty.** arXiv math.NT listings Oct–Nov 2026 (only 2610.21511 classifies), Zenodo ("class number" AND "imaginary quadratic"; Jaswal's conditional h ≤ 3000 is the only classification), HAL (nothing beyond small h), github.com/iwaokimura/cln-imq-2k (no run beyond 2000), our files. New as far as we can see.

**Caveat.** The analytic step is our reviewed paper proof (Littlewood78), not Lean-checked; the tables are trusted as published; the full list is not stored (rerun.md recomputes it: about 3 h of download, 53 GB streamed, and 3.7 h of sieving).

**RH relevance:** N11 (the class number problem is the effective shadow of Landau–Siegel zeros; here made effective by the Lean-checked zero-free half-plane).
