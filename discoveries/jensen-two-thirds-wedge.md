# Two-thirds hyperbolicity wedge for Riemann's Jensen polynomials with an explicit constant (node N6)

**Claim.** Let J^{d,n} be the degree-d Jensen polynomial of Riemann's Xi shifted by n. J^{d,n} is hyperbolic whenever n² log(n+2) ≥ 24 d³, for every d ≥ 1 and n ≥ 0, with d distinct negative zeros when d > 9.0·10^24 (degrees d ≤ 9.0·10^24 are covered by the known all-n range from Chasse's sector lemma and RH to height 3·10^12; our recorded range d ≤ 1.6141·10^25 also works). Without that range: n² log(n+2) ≥ 2.76·10^25 d³ gives d distinct negative zeros for every d ≥ 1. This is the largest explicit hyperbolic region known: all n ≥ (24 d³/log n)^{1/2}.

**Before.** A. Savva (Zenodo 22293642, 3 Sep 2026, unrefereed, AI-assisted) states the two-thirds wedge n² log(n+2) ≥ K d³ with K "effective in the usual proof-theoretic sense" but prints no number (C_B6 and N_analytic symbolic, TeX l. 95-101, App l. 826-831); his Lean development fixes the cutoff n ≥ e^(10^15), so its implied constant is at least e^(3·10^15). Our previous explicit region was n³ log²(n+2) ≥ 7000 d⁵ (Holland's wedge, [page](jensen-wedge-constant.md)); the new region contains it for every d > 9.0·10^24 (at d = 10^25 the n-threshold drops from about 4.5·10^41 to 1.7·10^37). Holland (arXiv 2608.08682), Griffin-Ono-Rolen-Zagier and Kim-Lee are ineffective for large d; Planat (HAL hal-05675741) fits a constant numerically.

**Method.** Savva's architecture is kept: six matched coefficients between J^{d,n} and a comparison 3F2, written as a multiplicative finite free convolution of two Jacobi polynomials (real roots by Marcus-Spielman-Srivastava and Mirabelli-Marcus-Peters), Gershgorin localization of its roots, a six-node complex Hermite-Genocchi bound for the coefficient ratio, and multiplier stability. His analytic input (a sectorial saddle asymptotic with existential constants) is replaced by our explicit complex saddle bound on |z − n| ≤ n/10 (from the Holland wedge work, extended to the sixth derivative), and every unquantified step by Arb enclosures: the matching branch by a Brouwer fixed-point certificate in each of 1000 cells (2210 for the self-contained form), localization constant 4.0006 instead of 12 + 8√6, critical-radius constant 2.3 instead of 4096. The audit found no mathematical error in Savva's argument (a misprinted expanded recurrence coefficient, App l. 688, is not used). Details: Riemann/Deep/SavvaK/DERIVATION.md, AUDIT.md.

**Certificate.** `cd Riemann/Deep/SavvaK && uv run --no-project --with python-flint --with sympy python3 certify6.py 80 2.3 1000 1.33` (35 s: K_res ≤ 23.0332, K_N(9e24) ≤ 5.6413, "K <= 24: PASS") and `... certify6.py 24 4 400 1.33 -self` (2 min: K_self ≤ 2.756·10^25). The orchestrator reran both on a copy (6 Nov 2026): logs and per-cell witnesses identical. Sanity: all 319 J^{d,n} with d ≤ 30, n ≤ 300 hyperbolic by Arb root isolation, including wedge-boundary pairs; at n = 10^40 on the boundary the true residual is 0.7998 against the certified 0.8254 (need ≤ 0.8633).

**Review.** The agent's reviewer: ERROR (six points), then GAP; all repaired (Brouwer with a uniform error bound in place of Krawczyk on an approximate map, per-cell witnesses written out, positivity and continuity of the exact matching map). Orchestrator's verdict-only reviewer: CORRECT, conditional on the cited analytic input and the reproduced Arb enclosures (Sec. 3 Brouwer argument valid, simplicity via critical-point signs, assembly K_res < 23.04 < 24, K_N < 5.65); one printed value corrected (λ⁵(λ − 1) = 1.37332; θ_E = 0.86329 was computed from the correct value); SIGNIFICANT.

**Novelty.** arXiv listings Oct-Nov 2026 (math.NT, math.CA, math.CV; lst.py 'Jensen|hyperbolic|Turán|Laguerre'): no explicit Jensen region (2610.38870 is a GRH criterion at shift 0). Zenodo '"Jensen polynomials"': Savva 22293642 (no number), the rest RH claims, conditional or small-degree. HAL: Planat hal-05675741 (fitted constant, not rigorous). Our files: only the weaker Holland wedge.

**Caveat.** Cited, not re-proved: the MSS max-root theorem, Mirabelli-Marcus-Peters' propositions, Jacobi zeros as Jacobi-matrix eigenvalues, Chasse's lemma with the verified RH height, Pólya's integral; the saddle bound was reviewed in three rounds in the Holland work. Python and Arb, not formalized; Savva's Lean development was not built here. Statement 1 gives only hyperbolicity for d ≤ 9.0·10^24.

**RH relevance:** N6 (RH ⟺ every J^{d,n} is hyperbolic): the explicit hyperbolic region grows from n ≳ d^{5/3} to n ≳ d^{3/2} up to logarithms; the barrier is uniformity in d, which is RH itself.
