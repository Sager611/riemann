# Small gaps between zeta zeros: the floor of Lamzouri's criterion and of the pair-correlation LP

**Claim.** Let D(λ) be the lower density of consecutive normalised gaps (γ_{n+1} − γ_n)·log T/2π that are ≤ λ. Lamzouri's criterion (arXiv 2611.14486, Thm Main2, TeX l. 392–400: 1/2 < θ < 4/7, f ∈ C_c² even, f ≥ 0, f^(0) = 1, f^(u) ≤ 0 for |u| > λ, J_θ(f) = f(0) + 2∫_0^θ f(α)α dα > 1 ⇒ D(λ) > 0) can never prove D(λ) > 0 for any λ ≤ 0.775, with any θ < 4/7; the same linear program with θ = 1 (Montgomery's pair correlation under RH, the method of Chirre–Gonçalves–de Laat) can never reach λ ≤ 0.595. So the unconditional record λ ≥ 0.7851 (ours, smallgap) is within 0.0101 of what the method can ever give; float LPs put that floor near 0.784. Under RH the θ = 1 functional stops at 0.595 (best value 0.6039, Chirre–Gonçalves–de Laat), while Durkan–Pearce–Crump (arXiv 2610.28527) prove D(λ) > 0 for every λ > 1/2 under RH by another argument, so the RH case is not capped: their method uses more than this functional.

**Before.** No floor for this linear program was known: Chirre–Gonçalves–de Laat (1810.08843, Thm 4) optimise numerically and say their verification does not establish optimality; Lagarias–Rodgers (1905.12123) show that band-limited correlations under RH are compatible with a process whose gaps are all ≥ 1/2 (the Alternative-Hypothesis obstruction 1/2); Carneiro–Chandee–Chirre–Milinovich (2108.09258) state no small-gap limit above 1/2; Lamzouri writes that further optimisation may improve his 0.7864/0.7892; our smallgap agent had no dual bound. Under RH, Durkan–Pearce–Crump (2610.28527) reach every λ > 1/2, the Alternative-Hypothesis limit. For the different problem μ = liminf of normalised gaps, the Montgomery–Odlyzko method's limit 0.508 is known (Inoue–Kobayashi–Toma 2510.14309, quoted in 2604.05733).

**Method.** (explore34; Riemann/Deep/ExploreDPP/DERIVATION.md.) Lemma 1 (weak duality): a measure σ = Σ_{k≠0} h(1 + η_|k|)δ_{kh} ≥ 0 with no mass in (−λ, λ) whose structure factor P(α) = (1 − h) + 2hΣ η_k cos(2παkh) is ≥ |α|·1[|α| ≤ θ] on [0, 1/(2h)] (θ ≤ 1/(2h)) gives J_θ(f) ≤ f^(0) for every admissible f (Poisson summation; σ is a pseudo pair-correlation measure with hard core λ). Theorem A: the 1/(1+θ)-discrete sine determinantal process (η_k = −sinc²(ka)) has P = 1 − Σ_m Δ(α − m/a) = |α| on [−θ, θ], so no λ ≤ 1/(1+θ) is reachable (1/2 at θ = 1, the Alternative Hypothesis; 7/11 for θ < 4/7). Theorem B: LP-optimised lattice duals, certified in Arb, push this to 0.775 (θ ≤ 4/7) and 0.595 (θ = 1). The dual's shape is an AH-like pseudo-lattice with spacing ≈ λ; the real zeros do not look like it (about 31 % of their gaps are ≤ 0.76 at heights 10^8–10^11).

**Certificate.** verify_dual.py (python-flint, 360 bits; rational η with η_k = −1 exactly for k < k0; P and P′ at every grid point plus θ and 1/(2h); cell minorant min(g, g + g′δ) − M2δ²/2 with M2 = 2hΣ|η_k|(2πkh)² ≥ |P″|; g = P − α left of θ, P right of θ): θ = 4/7, λ = 0.775, h = 31/280, k0 = 7, K = 235: min G ≥ 0.000204 on 90,324 cells; θ = 1, λ = 0.595, h = 119/600, k0 = 3, K = 202: min G ≥ 0.000239 on 100,841 cells (logs/dual_t47_k7X26.json, logs/cert_t1k3_X40_lam0.595.json).

**Caveat.** A statement about one method (the band-limited LP with Lamzouri's functional, unconditional θ < 4/7 or RH θ = 1), not about D(λ): a hard-core inequality on consecutive gaps, summed along Lamzouri's gap-free set, crosses this floor (D(λ) > 0 for λ ≥ 0.768, [small-gap-hardcore](small-gap-hardcore.md)); mollifier, moment and resonance arguments are not covered, and the zeros themselves have many small gaps. It carries no RH information. **RH relevance:** N8 (gaps between zeros): locates where the unconditional pair-correlation route to small gaps stops (the record can move by at most 0.0101 with it) and shows that the θ = 1 functional is strictly weaker than Durkan–Pearce–Crump's RH argument.
