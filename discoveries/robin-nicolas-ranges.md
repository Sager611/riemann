# Robin's and Nicolas' inequalities verified far beyond earlier ranges (node N4)

**Claim.** Robin's inequality σ(n) < e^γ n log log n holds for every 5041 ≤ n ≤ 10^(10^46.79), i.e. for every colossally abundant number whose largest prime is at most 1.43·10^47. Nicolas' inequality N_k/φ(N_k) > e^γ log log N_k holds for every primorial N_k with p_k ≤ 1.78·10^48. Inputs: RH to height 3·10^12 (Platt–Trudgian), Chirre–Helfgott's pointwise bound |ψ(x) − x| ≤ πx/(3·10^12) + 113.67√x (arXiv 2512.15709v1, a preprint; it needs only RH to that height) and the Lean-checked zero-free half-plane Re s > 0.87338196661320752 (Bebel, QuasiRiemannTracker). With 7/8: 10^(10^46.79) and 1.76·10^48; without any half-plane: 10^(10^46.44) and 6.30·10^47 (robinch, 5 Nov 2026). Previous version (robinh, R344): 10^(10^38.71) and 1.31·10^40.

**Before.** Ours: 10^(10^38.71) and p_k ≤ 1.31·10^40 (robinh, R344). Robin: Polak (Zenodo 21808589, Aug 2026, unrefereed) n ≤ 10^(10^22.85) (largest prime 1.65·10^23); refereed: Morrill–Platt (arXiv 1809.10813) n ≤ 10^(10^13.11485). Nicolas: no verified height-tied range; floating-point computations to p_k ≤ 10^9 (Nicolas 2012, arXiv 1202.0729).

**Method.** Both inequalities reduce to two-sided bounds on the tail integral −I(x) = ∫_x^∞ (t − θ(t))(1 + log t)/(t² log² t) dt (Rosser–Schoenfeld's Mertens identity), whose explicit formula has zero weights about 1/(ρ(ρ − 1)). Inputs: RH to height 3·10^12 (Platt–Trudgian), no zeros with Re s > 7/8 (OpenAI 2026; Lean port in Riemann/Deep/QRH), the explicit zero count of Hasanalizade–Shen–Wong, Büthe's bounds below 10^19, Morrill–Platt below 10^(10^13.11). New step (Level 2): the cutoff at x is smoothed linearly in log t over a window of log-width λ ≈ 10^−9, so zeros above the verified height cost x^(β−1)/(λγ³) instead of x^(β−1)/γ²; the window costs a pointwise bound η ≈ 4·10^−11 on |θ(x)/x − 1| (our Lemma 10_k, k-fold box averages of ψ). The smoothing alone gives 10^(10^32.98); the 7/8 half-plane adds a factor of about 4·10^5 in the prime range. Level 3 (robinch, [RobinCH](../Riemann/Deep/RobinCH/REPORT.md)): η is Chirre–Helfgott's bound (Lemma 12, η ≈ 1.05·10^−12; this alone gives 10^(10^40.56)); a centred k-box ramp (Lemma 11_k, k ≤ 57) damps the zeros above 3·10^12 by (2k/(λγ))^k at a window cost η times the ramp's mean absolute deviation; Lemma 13 sharpens Robin's second-order term to η²/(2 log x)(1 + …). The limit is now X ~ (T0²/c)², almost independent of the half-plane.
