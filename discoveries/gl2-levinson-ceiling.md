**Method.** (gl2ceil; Riemann/Deep/GL2Ceil/DERIVATION.md.) Complex Q never helps with real P, so Q ranges over the real class Q(0) = 1, Q(y) + Q(1 − y) ≡ β. With w = e^{Ry}Q, c = (1 + w(1)²)/2 + (C_P/ξ)∫w² + ξB_P∫w′², B_P = ∫P², C_P = ∫P′². For fixed Q the unique minimiser over P is sinh(λx)/sinh λ (Conrey), so the ceiling is sup over (R, λ) of 1 − (1/R) log M(R, λ), M the minimum over Q of a positive quadratic functional; B_λ decreases and C_λ increases in λ, and the ceiling increases with ξ. Lemma D (weak duality): for every continuous piecewise polynomial σ and every μ symmetric about 1/2, M(R, λ) ≥ 1/2 + ∫μ − 2σ(0) − (2σ(1) + e^{−R}∫μ)²/2 − (ξ/C_λ)∫(σ′ + μe^{−Ry})² − (1/(ξB_λ))∫σ² (pointwise Fenchel bounds, integration by parts, folding y ↔ 1 − y); at the Euler–Lagrange solution the duality gap vanishes.
