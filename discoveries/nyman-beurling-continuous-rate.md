# Continuous Nyman–Beurling distance: the exact rate under RH alone (node N5)

**Claim.** Assume RH. Let D(λ) = dist_{L²(0,∞)}(1_{(0,1)}, span{ {θ/x} : λ ≤ θ ≤ 1 }) and L = log(1/λ). Then L·D(λ)² → Σ_ρ m_ρ²/|ρ|² as λ → 0 (sum over the distinct nontrivial zeros, m_ρ the multiplicity; = 2 + γ − log 4π = 0.0461… if all zeros are simple). Nyman's constrained variant has the same limit. No hypothesis on ζ′(ρ) is used.

**Before.** The lower bound liminf L·D(λ)² ≥ Σ m_ρ²/|ρ|² holds unconditionally (Burnol, arXiv math/0103058; Báez-Duarte–Balazard–Landreau–Saias 2000 for simple zeros); the matching limit was a conjecture (BBLS; Burnol, math/0112254: "conjecture that equality holds (also with lim in place of liminf)"). For the discrete distance d_N the best RH-only upper bound is d_N² ≪ (log log N)^{5/2+δ}/(log N)^{1/2} (Balazard–de Roton, arXiv 0812.1689), and d_N² ~ C/log N is known only under RH plus Σ_{|γ|≤T}|ζ′(ρ)|^{−2} ≪ T^{3/2−δ} (Bettin–Conrey–Farmer, arXiv 1211.5191). Our explore59 (20 Nov 2026) had reduced the continuous rate to an interpolation cost at the zeros, with two open lemmas.

**Method.** (i) Unconditional reduction (explore59, its contour step repaired by dbarnb §6): D(λ)² ≤ (1 + L^{−1/2})·U_{Z(T₂)}(L − √L) + O(L^{−3/2}), T₂ ∈ [L⁶, L⁶ + 4], where U_Z(ℓ) is the cost of interpolating 1 at the zeros below T₂ (with multiplicities) in a one-sided Ornstein–Uhlenbeck Paley–Wiener space; the zeros above T₂ are removed by Titchmarsh 9.7 rectangle tilings and the divided difference (ζ(s) − ζ(w))/(s − w), with no bound on 1/ζ′(ρ). (ii) Exact dual form: U_Z(ℓ) = sup |p(0)|²/N_ℓ(p) over exponential sums (with jets) whose frequencies are the ordinates, N_ℓ the OU norm: a weighted Nikolskii inequality. (iii) Under RH the ordinates are real and S(t) ≪ log t/log log t, so a group of ordinates with gaps < L^{−3/4} has M ≪ log γ/log log γ members; Turán–Nazarov (no growth factor for real frequencies) and a reverse Bernstein step give |p_g(0)|² ≤ e^{O(M)}(ℓγ_g²)^{−1}∫|p_g′|²χ for each group; Gevrey-2 windows make the groups almost orthogonal (leak e^{−cL^{1/8}}); Cauchy–Schwarz gives sup_L L·U < ∞ (Lemma R″) and tail domination (Lemma R′), hence lim L·U = Σ m²/|ρ|² (explore59's Claim F for the low zeros). (iv) Burnol's liminf closes the limit.

**Certificate.** None: an analytic, ineffective theorem. Numerics are falsification only: the OU Gram equals explore59's kernel matrix to 10⁻¹⁶ (orchestrator's rerun of dbarnb_falsify.py dual from a copy: identical); RH-allowed worst-case clusters (every 8th zero replaced by an unresolved cluster of 4–6 ordinates) keep the cost within a factor 1.10 of the decoupled sum at L = 10 and 1.001 at L ≥ 40, with L·U bounded.
