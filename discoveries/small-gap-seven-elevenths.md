# Durkan–Pearce-Crump's small-gap method past length T^{1/2} without RH: D(λ) > 0 for every λ > 7/11

**Claim.** (Theorem U′.) Let κ > 0 be a uniform density slope, N(σ, 3T) ≪ T^{1−κ(σ−½)} log T for all σ ≥ ½. For every φ ∈ (max{2/(2+κ), ½}, 1) there is c(φ) > 0 such that for all large T, #{n : T ≤ γ_n ≤ γ_{n+1} ≤ 2T, γ_{n+1} − γ_n < 2πφ/log T} ≥ c(φ) T log T (ordinates with multiplicity, as in Theorem U). With Conrey's slope κ = 8/7 − δ (proved in Baluyot's thesis): D(λ) > 0 for every λ > 7/11 = 0.6364, unconditionally.

**Before.** Unconditional: λ > 2/3 (ours, Theorem U, dpcuncond, 12 Nov 2026, [small-gap-two-thirds](small-gap-two-thirds.md)), 0.768 (ours, explore40, [small-gap-hardcore](small-gap-hardcore.md)), 0.7892 (Lamzouri, arXiv 2611.14486). Under RH: 0.6878 (Soundararajan), 0.6553 (Wu), 0.6039 (Bui–Goldston–Milinovich–Montgomery), 1/2 (Durkan–Pearce-Crump, arXiv 2610.28527, Thm B). Theorem U's page called the needed (2 + δ)-th moment of R_T beyond length T^{1/2} open (Montgomery–Vaughan gives T^{2θ}).

**Method.** (dpcbeyond; [PROOF.md](../Riemann/Deep/DPCBeyond/PROOF.md).) Theorem U ([PROOF.md](../Riemann/Deep/DPCUncond/PROOF.md)) verbatim at mollifier length X = T^θ, θ ∈ (1/φ − 1, min{κ/2, 1}), except that Montgomery–Vaughan's fourth moment (Step 5a, its only θ < ½ input) is replaced by: (Lemma Z) a pointwise bound through the zeros, |R_T(t − iy)| ≤ C(1 + W_{−y})(1 + On)^{k−1} and |R*(t − iy)| ≤ C X^{y}(1 + W)(1 + On)^{k−1} for t ∈ [T/2, 3T], 0 ≤ y ≤ ½, with On = Σ_ρ κ_A(L|γ − t|) the smoothed local zero count and W = Σ_ρ X^{(β−½)⁺} κ_A(L|γ − t|); proof: the multivariate explicit formula for the prime factors of n with DPC's cutoff f(Σ log q_i/L) kept inside the r-fold integral, so an r-fold product costs X^{max d}, not X^{r·max d} (DPC's RH route |e_r| ≪ (1 + |S_1|)^r loses exactly this); (Lemma M) depth bins of off-line zeros (≤ C TL e^{−κa} zeros at depth a/L) and Fujii's moments give ∫_T^{2T}|R_T|^q ≪ T for every q < κ/θ, so q > 2 for θ < κ/2, and the same over the zeros (Lamzouri's DoubleSumZeros); (6e′) the hairpins of Theorem U bounded pointwise by Lemma Z, e^{2θa} per depth bin against e^{−κa}: 2θ < κ; (7′) Hölder with ‖R_T‖_q, q > 2, in place of ‖R_T‖₄. DPC's sign condition φ(1 + θ) > 1 then allows every φ > max{2/(2 + κ), ½}; Jutila's κ = 1 − δ gives back 2/3: the whole gain is Conrey's mollifier length 4/7, imported through the density theorem, without a mean value theorem for R_T longer than T^{1/2}.
