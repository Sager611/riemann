# Zeros on the critical line for combinations of three Dirichlet L-functions: at least 2.06% (nodes N8, N12)

**Claim.** Let q ≥ 3, χ_1, …, χ_N distinct primitive characters mod q of the same parity, b_j nonzero reals and e^{2iθ_j} = i^a τ̄(χ_j)/√q, so that F = Σ_j b_j e^{iθ_j} L(s, χ_j) has a functional equation but no Euler product (the Davenport–Heilbronn family). Unconditionally,
liminf_{T→∞} N_0^{odd}(F; T)/N_F(T) ≥ 1 − Σ_j min(1 − s₀, 1/N), s₀ = 1669159/2478195 = 0.6735382,
where N_0^{odd} counts the distinct zeros on the critical line of odd multiplicity. So **N = 3: at least 51087/2478195 = 2.061%** of the zeros lie on the line (was 4.91·10⁻⁹); **N = 2: at least 860123/2478195 = 34.707%** (was ours 34.498%; Bombieri–Hejhal's 34.5% needed GRH); N ≥ 4: nothing without a new lemma. A weaker route that uses only the inputs of our earlier reviewed N = 2 result gives N = 3: ≥ 3p − 2 = 1.747% (p = 0.672491294). By-product (Lemma M): at least 67.35% of the zeros of ζ and of each fixed L(s, χ) are simple, on the line and isolated from the other zeros on the line at some scale κ > 0.

**Before.** N = 3: Dousselin (arXiv 2311.10285 = Forum Math. 37 (2025) 1871–1912; Selberg's method made explicit): κ_F ≥ 4.91·10⁻⁹ (3.68·10⁻⁹ for N = 4, about 2.161·10⁻⁶/(N log N) for large N). N = 2: ours 0.3449825 ([linear-combinations-line](linear-combinations-line.md)); Lamzouri 2610.26841v2 Thm 1.4: 31%; Bombieri–Hejhal (Duke 1995): 34.5% under GRH. Lamzouri's remark needs δ_1 + δ_2 > 1, so N = 3 needs isolated proportions above 2/3, out of reach of 2610.26841's own constants. (Our round R401 wrongly recorded N ≥ 3 as having only Selberg's non-explicit proportion.)

**Method.** (1) Proposition N: Lamzouri's unconditional Bombieri–Hejhal argument for N functions (joint Selberg central limit theorem, a separation set over all pairs, dominance of the largest |L_j| over its N − 1 rivals, sign changes of F's Hardy function forced by isolated zeros of the dominant L_j; capacity: each L_j dominates on 1/N of the blocks). (2) Only isolation from the other zeros on the line matters, since off-line zeros do not change the sign of the Hardy function. (3) Lemma M: merging κ-clusters of real points into multiple points changes the Hilbert–Schmidt norm of the zero operator of arXiv 2610.08965 by O(κ√N), so its Lean-checked simple-zero certificate (67.3538%) also counts simple zeros κ-isolated from the other zeros on the line.
