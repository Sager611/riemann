# At least 7.005% of the zeros of every GL(2) newform L-function lie on the critical line (nodes N8, N12)

**Claim.** For every holomorphic or Hecke–Maass newform f on Γ₀(N), any level N, the proportion κ_f of nontrivial zeros of L(s, f) on the critical line satisfies κ_f > 0.07005 (certified κ_f ≥ 0.0700562390188), unconditionally; κ_f > 0.08971 (≥ 0.0897101789336) under the generalised Ramanujan conjecture for the Maass spectrum. At Bernard's shorter length ν < 5/27: κ_f > 0.06642. Also: Question 5 of arXiv 2508.11108 is true: κ(θ) > 2θ/3 for every θ ∈ (0, 1/2] for that paper's construction (P = x, R = √(3/5)/θ), which arXiv 2607.00282 uses at all its lengths while 2508.11108 proved it only below a non-explicit θ₀.

**Before.** 6.96% unconditional and 8.96% under Ramanujan (Andersen–Thorner, arXiv 2004.03581, Forum Math. 2021; their published parameters give 0.0696049654 and 0.0896556716 in our Arb code); 6.32% at Bernard's length (2508.11108, P = x; Bernard 2015: 2.97%).

**Method.** Conrey's mollified mean value for GL(2) (Bernard 1404.6432 Thm th:mm2; Andersen–Thorner Thm thm:nu extends the length to ν < 1/4 − θ_KS/2 = 25/128, and to 1/4 under Ramanujan) and the zero count κ_f ≥ 1 − (1/R) log c(P, Q, R, ν/2) for Q in Bernard's class. The agent optimised P and Q jointly: for fixed Q the best P is sinh(λx)/sinh λ, λ = ξ√(W₁/W₀) (Conrey §4); for fixed P the functional is a positive quadratic in Q (Ritz with 30 odd Chebyshev terms in 1 − 2y, Q(0) + Q(1) free); alternating steps plus a search in R. The final parameters are rounded to rationals; P is the degree-25 Taylor polynomial of sinh(λx) normalised to P(1) = 1, so P(0) = 0, P(1) = 1 and Q(0) = 1, Q′(y) = Q′(1 − y) hold exactly. Question 5: the functional reduces to c(S) = 1/2 − √(3/5)/3 + (2/√15)e^R I_R(S); a near-optimal S on [0, R_a], extended by 0 (admissible in H¹), covers every R ≥ R_a, and three nodes R_a = 1.5491, 2.0386, 3.3906 cover θ ∈ (0, 1/2]. Derivation with source line numbers: [DERIVATION.md](../Riemann/Deep/Degree2Mol/DERIVATION.md).

**Certificate.** `cd Riemann/Deep/Degree2Mol && uv run --no-project --with python-flint --with mpmath python3 cert.py && uv run --no-project --with python-flint --with mpmath python3 q5cert.py` (52 s and 1 s, 61 MB, one thread). cert.py evaluates the original double integral exactly over Q (the y-integral by the finite formula ∫₀¹ e^{ay}G = Σ_k (−1)^k (e^a G^{(k)}(1) − G^{(k)}(0))/a^{k+1}); the only roundings are exp and log in Arb at 4000 bits, outward; κ is an Arb ball compared with the threshold. Lengths ξ = 25/256 − 10⁻⁷, 1/8 − 10⁻⁷, 5/54 − 10⁻⁷, strictly inside the open ranges.

**Caveat.** The gains are small (+0.045 and +0.005 percentage points) and come from a standard optimisation; most of it is convergence in the degree of Q. All numbers rest on the quoted mean value theorems, which were not re-proved here. 7.0056% looks like the ceiling of the one-piece Levinson–Conrey method at ν = 25/128 (numerically converged, not proved). Routine side items (COROLLARIES): the twist families of 2607.00282 on average, 0.1199 (PGL(3), stated 1/9) and 0.3658 / 0.3562 simple (PGL(2), stated 1/3); and two entries of 2508.11108's table are wrong (κ(2/3) = 0.44636, κ(1/2) = 0.35843, printed 0.364…, 0.334…; still valid lower bounds).

**RH relevance:** N8, N12: critical-line proportions are the quantitative form of RH for a given L-function; this raises the unconditional proportion for the whole GL(2) family, the first degree-2 improvement since 2021.
