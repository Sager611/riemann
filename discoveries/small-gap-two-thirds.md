# Durkan–Pearce-Crump's small-gap method without RH: D(λ) > 0 for every λ > 2/3

**Claim.** (Theorem U.) For every φ ∈ (2/3, 1) there is c(φ) > 0 such that for all large T, #{n : T ≤ γ_n ≤ γ_{n+1} ≤ 2T, γ_{n+1} − γ_n < 2πφ/log T} ≥ c(φ) T log T (ordinates counted with multiplicity, an off-line pair giving two equal ordinates, as in Lamzouri 2611.14486). Hence D(λ) > 0 for every λ > 2/3, unconditionally.

**Before.** Unconditional: λ ≥ 0.768 (ours, explore40, the same day, [small-gap-hardcore](small-gap-hardcore.md)), 0.7851 (ours, smallgap), 0.7892 (Lamzouri, arXiv 2611.14486). Under RH: λ > 0.6878 (Soundararajan), 0.6553 (Wu), 0.6039 (Bui–Goldston–Milinovich–Montgomery), 1/2 (Durkan–Pearce-Crump, arXiv 2610.28527, Thm B). Round R448 expected at best 7/11 from a Lamzouri-type removal and called it open.

**Method.** (dpcuncond; [PROOF.md](../Riemann/Deep/DPCUncond/PROOF.md).) DPC's contradiction argument: two-line moments ∫G_T|R_T|²P(V_φ), V_φ the window difference of log ξ, P = q² + i b₀ with Re P ≥ 0 wherever Im V ∈ {0, 1}, shifted from the critical line to Re s = 3/2, where everything is an Euler series. Without RH: (1) log ξ is continued horizontally from Re s = +∞, so Im V counts all zeros (an off-line pair twice); (2) under the contradiction hypothesis there are ≤ 2εTL off-line zeros (each pair is a zero gap); (3) each off-line zero β + iγ cuts a vertical slit of depth β − 1/2 into the shift rectangle, and the exact contour identity adds 'hairpin' integrals along the slits (coincident cuts merged by their total jump); (4) on a slit, R_T R*(x − iy) pairs a factor at 1/2 + y with one at the mirror point 1/2 − y, so an off-line zero costs T^{θ(β−1/2)}, not T^{2θ(β−1/2)}; sparse-kernel Hölder estimates (Poisson representations from the line and from Im t = −Y, the zero-removed ξ̂, Fujii's moments) bound the hairpin total by Φ(ε)T with Φ(ε) → 0, given Jutila's density slope 1 − δ; (5) DPC's RH Lemma 5 (Rodgers' moments) is replaced by the Montgomery–Vaughan fourth moment of R_T, valid at length T^θ for θ < 1/2, and unconditional L^m bounds for V (M. Riesz, Poisson smearing, Fujii, density). DPC's sign condition φ(1 + θ) > 1 then allows every φ > 2/3. At length T^θ the method's exact limit is 1/(1 + θ) (⟨1, (I + aS)⁻¹1⟩ = tan(aθ)/a).
