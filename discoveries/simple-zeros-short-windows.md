# A positive proportion of simple critical zeros in every window (T, T + T^θ], θ > 1/2

**Claim.** (Theorem E1.) For every fixed θ ∈ (1/2, 1), liminf_{T→∞} S(T, T^θ)/N(T, T^θ) ≥ h(θ; κ₁) ≥ h(1/2; κ₁) = 0.0055353042386 > 0, where N(T, H) counts the zeros of ζ with T < γ ≤ T + H (with multiplicity) and S(T, H) the simple ones with β = 1/2; h(θ; k) = (3 + k − √((1 − k)(9 − k − 8c(θ))))/4, c(θ) = 2 − θ/2 − cot(θ/√2)/√2 (Wang), κ₁ = 0.0844779079697 (a Levinson detector at mollifier length ν = 3/25). Unconditional, given the inputs below. The moment part (Theorem E1-M): Santibáñez-Leal's short-window shifted mollified moment holds for every mollifier length ν < (3θ − 1)/4. Below θ = 1/2 the method stops on both sides (the moment needs θ > 37/75 at ν = 3/25, the detector κ(ν) > k*(θ) needs θ > 0.49384, and the Taylor step needs θ > 1/2).

**Before.** θ ≥ 0.527 (Santibáñez-Leal, Zenodo 23134787 v0.03, 4 Oct 2026, unrefereed; audited PASSED by us, [SantiAudit](../Riemann/Deep/SantiAudit/REPORT.md)); θ > 0.5458837 (Santibáñez-Leal, Zenodo 22860012 v0.07); θ > θ₀ = 0.5501940 (Wang, arXiv 2609.07918, localized Montgomery + Lamzouri); θ ≥ 0.552 (Steuding, Acta Math. Hungar. 96 (2002), Levinson with linear Q). For zeros on the line of odd order (not simple), Karatsuba reaches θ > 27/82; full-interval proportions (Conrey, Bui–Conrey–Young, PRZZ) say nothing about short windows.

**Method.** (santiaudit; [E1.md](../Riemann/Deep/SantiAudit/E1.md).) Only §3.5 of Santibáñez-Leal's proof changes. His exact transformation of the off-diagonal (Estermann's functional equation) leaves an oscillatory dual sum over n of 𝒯(n) = (2πi)⁻¹∫ Γ(u − d/2)Γ(u + d/2)cos(πu)(pq/(4π²n))^u Ã(1 − u − z/2) du; he bounds it by Cauchy–Schwarz in t (ν < min{1/2, 2θ − 1, (5θ − 2)/6}). E1 rewrites 𝒯(n) in x-space as a Bessel integral −π∫A(ξ)ξ^{−z/2}B(4π√(nξ/(pq)))dξ (Mellin–Parseval, DLMF 10.22.43–44), uses the Hankel form of B (no stationary point), and integrates by parts against the width-H Gaussian profile: |𝒯(n)| ≪ H(pq/(nT))^{1/4}(1 + √(n/N₀))^{−K}, N₀ = pqT/H². Summing absolutely, the dual is ≪ T^{1/2+ε}H^{−1/2}y², a relative error T^{1/2+2ν−3θ/2+ε}: power-small iff ν < (3θ − 1)/4. Steuding (2002) had this mollifier line only with linear Q; combined with Santibáñez-Leal's degree-201 detector (Q(y) + Q(1 − y) ≡ 1) and his parity transfer through Wang's pair sum, the positivity condition κ > k*(θ) = 1 − 2/(2 − c(θ)) holds down to θ = 1/2.

**Certificate.** e1_constants.py (exact rationals + Arb, 4000 bits): c(P, Q, R, ν) = 3302.2869232044 at ν = 3/25, R = 177/20; κ₁ = 0.0844779079697 (± 4·10⁻³²); c(1/2) = −0.1659638503650, k*(1/2) = 0.0766235550686; κ₁ − k*(1/2) = 0.0078543529 > 0; h(1/2; κ₁) = 0.0055353042386 (± 5·10⁻²⁸); with ν = 1249/10000, R = 173/20: h(1/2) = 0.0074290982. h increases in θ (c′ = ½cot²(θ/√2) > 0) and in κ.

**Novelty.** No source gives a positive proportion of simple critical zeros in windows T^θ for θ ≤ 0.527 (searched 12 Nov 2026: GXL evidence API, arXiv abstracts 'short intervals' with 'simple zeros', 'Levinson', 'critical line … proportion', Zenodo without a date filter, HAL, our files; E1.md §6 lists the classical sources). The ingredients are known (Steuding's line, Santibáñez-Leal's transformation, detector and parity transfer, Wang's pair sum); their combination reaching the method's barrier θ = 1/2 is new.

**Caveat.** Rests on two unrefereed preprints: Santibáñez-Leal's Zenodo 23134787 v0.03 (audited PASSED by us except Wang's lemma, attributed) and Wang's arXiv 2609.07918 (statement and range checked in its TeX, not refereed line by line); Estermann's functional equation and Young's main term are classical. The liminf is for each fixed θ (no rate, no θ = θ(T) ↓ 1/2). A line-by-line audit and a Lean formalization of the finite parts are running.
